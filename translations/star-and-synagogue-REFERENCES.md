# Reference list — look these up in your Bible

Companion to `star-and-synagogue-study.md` and `star-and-synagogue-crossrefs.md`.
Plain citations only. Every one was confirmed present in the Hebrew or Greek on disk.

⚠️ **Versification note:** Hebrew and English Bibles number a few Psalms differently.
Where that happens it is flagged. Everything else is the same number in both.

---

## PART 1 — Where the Bible condemns star / sun / moon / host-of-heaven worship

### The law
- **Deuteronomy 4:19** — the base prohibition. Sun, moon, stars = "all the host of heaven."
  Ends with the clause that God *allotted* them to all the peoples.
- **Deuteronomy 17:2–5** — makes it a capital offense. Penalty is stoning.

### The history books
- **2 Kings 17:16** — the northern kingdom's charge. Closest parallel to Amos.
- **2 Kings 21:3** — Manasseh rebuilds the high places, bows to all the host of heaven.
- **2 Kings 21:5** — builds altars to the host of heaven **inside the two courts of the temple**.
- **2 Kings 23:4** — Josiah removes the vessels made for the host of heaven and burns them.
- **2 Kings 23:5** — the fullest list anywhere: Baal, sun, moon, **the constellations/zodiac**,
  and all the host of heaven.
- **2 Kings 23:11–12** — **horses dedicated to the sun** removed, **chariots of the sun** burned,
  rooftop altars torn down.
- **2 Chronicles 33:3** — Chronicles' parallel to 2 Kings 21:3.
- **2 Chronicles 33:5** — parallel to 2 Kings 21:5.

### The prophets
- **Amos 5:26** — the passage under study.
- **Isaiah 47:13** — against Babylon. Astrologers, star-gazers, monthly forecasters, named
  outright and told to stand up and save her.
- **Jeremiah 7:18** — **the queen of heaven**. Children gather wood, fathers light fire,
  women knead cakes.
- **Jeremiah 8:2** — sun, moon, all the host of heaven. Five verbs: loved, served,
  walked after, sought, bowed down to.
- **Jeremiah 10:2** — "do not learn the way of the nations, and do not be dismayed at
  **the signs of the heavens**."
- **Jeremiah 19:13** — rooftop incense to all the host of heaven.
- **Jeremiah 44:17–19** — the queen of heaven again, and the **only place in the Bible where
  the people argue back and defend the practice**. They say they had plenty while they did it,
  and disaster came when they stopped.
- **Jeremiah 44:25** — God's reply to that argument.
- **Ezekiel 8:16** — about 25 men in the inner court of the temple, **backs to the temple,
  faces east, bowing to the sun**.
- **Zephaniah 1:5** — those who bow on the rooftops to the host of heaven.

### Wisdom
- **Job 31:26–28** — the subtlest one in the Bible. Seeing the moon, the heart **secretly
  enticed**, the hand kissed to the mouth. No altar, no image, no words — just a gesture.
  v. 28 still calls it a punishable iniquity and a denial of God.

### New Testament
- **Acts 7:42** — *read this one before 7:43.* "God turned and handed them over to serve
  **the host of heaven**." This is the hinge verse, and it's Deuteronomy 4:19 vocabulary.
- **Acts 7:43** — Stephen's quotation of Amos 5:26.

## PART 2 — The same words used in the opposite direction

Read these against Part 1. The Bible uses the identical vocabulary for the opposite point,
sometimes only a few verses away.

- **Amos 5:8** — *three verses before 5:26*: God as the maker of Pleiades and Orion.
- **Nehemiah 9:6** — "**and the host of heaven bow down to you**." The same verb that is the
  crime everywhere else in Part 1.
- **Genesis 2:1** — heaven and earth finished, "and all their host."
- **Psalm 33:6** — the host of heaven made by the breath of his mouth.
- **Isaiah 45:12** — "I commanded all their host."
- **Jeremiah 33:22** — the host of heaven as uncountable.
- **Isaiah 34:4** — all the host of heaven rots and rolls up like a scroll.
- **1 Kings 22:19** and **2 Chronicles 18:18** — the host of heaven standing at God's right
  and left. This is the divine council, not the pagan sky. (Keep reading: 1 Kings 22:20–22.)
- **Daniel 8:10** — the horn grows up to the host of heaven and casts down some of the host
  and of the stars.
- **Isaiah 14:12–13** — "shining one, son of the dawn," and "above the **stars of God** I will
  raise my throne."
- **Genesis 37:9** — sun, moon and eleven stars bowing to Joseph. The only place celestial
  bodies bow to a human and it is not counted as sin.
- **Deuteronomy 32:8** — worth reading directly after Deut 4:19. Both use the same
  *allotment* language. ⚠️ Note: the Hebrew text most Bibles translate here reads
  "sons of Israel," but the Dead Sea Scrolls and the Greek read "sons of God" — this is one of
  the verified findings already in the felz project.
- **Psalm 80:14** in English Bibles = **Psalm 80:15** in Hebrew — minor, listed only so the
  number doesn't confuse you if you compare.

## PART 3 — "Say they are Jews, and are not"

- **Revelation 2:9** — to Smyrna.
- **Revelation 3:9** — to Philadelphia.
- **Romans 2:28–29** — the outward Jew vs. the hidden one; circumcision of the heart.
  The closest parallel in the New Testament.
- **Romans 9:6** — "not all who are from Israel *are* Israel."
- **Philippians 3:3** — "**we** are the circumcision." A counter-claim to a title, the same
  move as Revelation 2:9.
- **John 8:39** and **John 8:44** — "if you were Abraham's children…" and the answer that
  follows. Same structure, much harsher.
- **Isaiah 1:10** — the prophet addresses Jerusalem's own rulers as **Sodom and Gomorrah**.
  This is the genre the Revelation passages belong to: insiders rebuking insiders.
- **Isaiah 60:14** — "they shall bow down at the soles of your feet." Confirmed on disk as the
  source of the image in Revelation 3:9.

## Reading order, if you want one

1. **Deuteronomy 4:19** → **Deuteronomy 17:2–5** — the rule and the penalty.
2. **2 Kings 17:16** → **Amos 5:26** — the charge against the northern kingdom.
3. **2 Kings 21:5** → **Ezekiel 8:16** — how far into the temple it got.
4. **2 Kings 23:4–12** — the purge, and the fullest list of what was being worshipped.
5. **Jeremiah 44:17–19** — the people's own defense of it, in their words.
6. **Job 31:26–28** — the version with no altar at all.
7. **Amos 5:8** then **Nehemiah 9:6** — the deliberate reversals.
8. **Acts 7:42–43** — the whole thing picked up and aimed at a live audience.
