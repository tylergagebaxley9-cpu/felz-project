## ⭐⭐⭐ THE APOCALYPSE OF WEEKS RUNS OUT OF ORDER — REPORTED, NOT REPAIRED

The Ge'ez, read straight through in the order the manuscripts give it, presents the ten weeks like this:

| Ge'ez location | Content |
|---|---|
| 91:1–10 | Enoch summons Methuselah; exhortation; "the righteous shall rise from sleep" |
| **91:11–17** | **weeks EIGHT, NINE, TEN, and the weeks without number** |
| 91:18–19 | the two ways |
| 92:1–5 | the superscription "written by Enoch" |
| **93:1–10** | **weeks ONE through SEVEN** |
| 93:11–14 | "who among the children of men is able…" |

🛑 **The second half of the vision stands eighty-odd verses before its first half.** Weeks 8–10 are narrated, the discourse moves on, a fresh superscription is given, and only then do weeks 1–7 begin — and having reached week seven, the text does not continue to week eight (it is already spent) but breaks into a hymn.

🛑 **I have not moved anything.** Reordering 91:11–17 to sit after 93:10 would produce a text that no manuscript in this apparatus contains. What is printed below is the order the Ge'ez actually has. The seam is marked where it falls.

⭐ Two seams are visible *inside* the Ge'ez itself, and they are the evidence, not my inference:

1. **93:9–10 ends week seven and never reaches week eight.** Instead 93:11 begins `እስመ፡ መኑ፡ ውእቱ፡ እምውሉደ፡ ሰብእ` — "for who is there among the children of men…". A sequence that has counted 1,2,3,4,5,6,7 stops there.
2. **91:12 opens with `ካልእት፡ ሰንበት፡ ሳምኒት`** — "*another* week, an eighth" — a phrase that presupposes a seventh has just been narrated. In the Ge'ez, nothing has been narrated; 91:11 was about roots and swords.

⚠️ What I will **not** do is tell you which order is original. The apparatus has no Aramaic and no Greek here at all, so there is nothing to arbitrate with.

| መቅሠፍት *mäqśäfətə* | *flagrum, flagellum, scutica* · *verber, plaga, vibex* · *castigatio divina, plaga, calamitas, poena a Deo inflicta* | (whip, scourge \| lash, welt \| divine chastisement, plague, calamity, punishment sent by God) |
| ሰንበት *sänəbätə* | *sabbatum, dies hebdomadis septimus* · *sabbatum Christianorum, dominica* · *sabbatum i. e. hebdomas, septimana* | (sabbath, the seventh day \| the Lord's day \| **week, a period of seven**) |

⭐ **The Apocalypse of Weeks counts *sabbaths*, not "weeks".** The Ge'ez word at 91:12, 93:3 and everywhere in the vision is ሰንበት — the ordinary word for the seventh day — used in Dillmann's third sense, *hebdomas*. The two senses are one word. A Ge'ez reader of "the eighth ሰንበት" hears "the eighth sabbath" and "the eighth week" simultaneously, and the sabbatical arithmetic of the vision is not a scholarly overlay on it — it is in the noun. **This is unrecoverable in English**, which has no word that is both.

## 1 Enoch 91 — Enoch summons his children · weeks eight, nine and ten

**Witnesses: Ge'ez alone.** No Greek, no Latin, no Aramaic, no Ethiopic apparatus for any verse of this chapter.

### 91:1

**ወይእዜኒ፡ ወልድየ፡ ማቱሳላ፡ ጸውዕ፡ ሊተ፡ ኵሎሙ፡ አኀዊከ፡ ወአስተጋብእ፡ ሊተ፡ ኵሎ፡ ደቂቀ፡ እምከ፡ እስመ፡ ቃል፡ ይጼውዐኒ፡ ወመንፈስ፡ ተክዕወ፡ በላዕሌየ፡ ከመ፡ አርእይክሙ፡ ኵሎ፡ ዘይበጽሐክሙ፡ እስከ፡ ለዓለም።**

*wäyəʾəzeni wälədəyä matusala ṣäwəʿə litä kwəlomu ʾäḫäwikä wäʾäsətägabəʾə litä kwəlo däqiqä ʾəməkä ʾəsəmä qalə yəṣewəʿäni wämänəfäsə täkəʿəwä bälaʿəleyä kämä ʾärəʾəyəkəmu kwəlo zäyəbäṣəḥäkəmu ʾəsəkä läʿalämə*

**And now, my son Methuselah — call to me all your brothers, and gather to me all the children of your mother; for a (voice | word) calls me, and a (spirit | wind | breath) has been poured out upon me, that I may show you all that (shall reach | shall befall | shall come upon) you unto eternity.**

- `ቃል` *qalə* — (voice | word | speech | sound). Ge'ez does not separate the utterance from the sound of it.
- `መንፈስ` *mänəfäsə* — (spirit | wind | breath). The verb with it is `ተክዕወ` *täkəʿəwä*, "was poured out", the verb of liquids.
- `ደቂቀ፡ እምከ` *däqiqä ʾəməkä* — literally "the children of **your mother**", not "your children". The line names Methuselah's siblings twice over: `አኀዊከ` (your brothers) and then the maternal formula.

### 91:2

**ወእምኔሁ፡ ሖረ፡ ማቱሳላ፡ ወጸውዖሙ፡ ለኵሎሙ፡ አኀዊሁ፡ ኀቤሁ፡ ወአስተጋብኦሙ፡ ለአዝማደ፡ ዚኣሁ።**

*wäʾəmənehu ḥorä matusala wäṣäwəʿomu läkwəlomu ʾäḫäwihu ḫäbehu wäʾäsətägabəʾomu läʾäzəmadä ziʾahu*

**And thereupon Methuselah went, and called all his brothers to him, and gathered his own kindred.**

- `አዝማድ` *ʾäzəmadə* — (kindred | kinsmen | family | relations). `ዚኣሁ` *ziʾahu* is the emphatic possessive, "of his own".

### 91:3

**ወተናገሮሙ፡ ለኵሎሙ፡ ውሉዱ፡ ጽድቅ፡ ወይቤ፡ ስምዑ፡ ደቂቅየ፡ ኵሎ፡ ነገረ፡ አቡክሙ፡ ወአጽምኡ፡ በርትዕ፡ ቃለ፡ አፉየ፡ እስመ፡ ኣሰምዕ፡ ዲቤክሙ፡ ወእነግረክሙ፡ ፍቁራንየ፡ አፍቅርዋ፡ ለርትዕ፡ ወባቲ፡ ሑሩ።**

*wätänagäromu läkwəlomu wəludu ṣədəqə wäyəbe səməʿu däqiqəyä kwəlo nägärä ʾäbukəmu wäʾäṣəməʾu bärətəʿə qalä ʾäfuyä ʾəsəmä ʾasäməʿə dibekəmu wäʾənägəräkəmu fəquranəyä ʾäfəqərəwa lärətəʿə wäbati ḥuru*

**And he spoke (righteousness | truth | what is right) to all his children, and said: Hear, my children, every word of your father, and give ear (rightly | uprightly | correctly) to the word of my mouth — for I bear witness (to you | upon you | against you) and I tell you, my beloved: love (uprightness | truth | what is fitting), and walk in her.**

- ⚠️ `ውሉዱ፡ ጽድቅ` *wəludu ṣədəqə* — the syntax is genuinely open. It can be read as an accusative of what he spoke ("he spoke righteousness to all his children") or as a bound phrase ("all his children of righteousness"). **I have not decided it.** Both readings are grammatical Ge'ez and nothing in the chapter settles it.
- `ኣሰምዕ፡ ዲቤክሙ` *ʾasäməʿə dibekəmu* — `ዲበ` is "upon / over / against". A witness borne *upon* someone in Ge'ez is as easily a witness *against* them. Enoch's testimony in this block is both, and the preposition refuses to choose.
- `ወባቲ፡ ሑሩ` — "and in **her** walk": `ርትዕ` is feminine, and the following verses keep the feminine pronoun for it. The path is personified as a woman throughout 91:3–4.

### 91:4

**ወኢትቅረቡ፡ ኀበ፡ ርትዕ፡ በክልኤ፡ ልብ፡ ወኢትኅበሩ፡ ምስለ፡ እለ፡ ክልኤ፡ ልብ፡ አላ፡ ሑሩ፡ በጽድቅ፡ ደቂቅየ፡ ወይእቲ፡ ትመርሐክሙ፡ በፍናዋት፡ ኄራት፡ ወጽድቅ፡ ይከውን፡ ለክሙ፡ ሱታፌ።**

*wäʾitəqəräbu ḫäbä rətəʿə bäkələʾe ləbə wäʾitəḫəbäru məsəlä ʾəlä kələʾe ləbə ʾäla ḥuru bäṣədəqə däqiqəyä wäyəʾəti təmärəḥäkəmu bäfənawatə ḫeratə wäṣədəqə yəkäwənə läkəmu sutafe*

**And do not draw near to (uprightness | truth) with a double heart, and do not join yourselves with those of a double heart; but walk in (righteousness | truth | integrity), my children, and she will lead you in good paths, and (righteousness | truth) shall be your (fellowship | partnership | companion | associate).**

- `በክልኤ፡ ልብ` *bäkəlʾe ləbə* — "with two heart(s)". The idiom is duality, not duplicity as such; Ge'ez says the number.
- ⭐ `ሱታፌ` *sutafe* — Dillmann gives 1) *communio, societas, communicatio*; 2) concrete *socius, consors, sodalis, collega, particeps*. So the clause is either "righteousness will be a **fellowship** for you" (abstract) or "righteousness will be your **companion**" (concrete), and Dillmann **cites this very verse** — `ጽድቅ ፡ ይከውን ለክሙ ፡ ሱታፌ፡ Hen. 91, 4` — under the concrete sense, without excluding the other.

### 91:5

**እስመ፡ ኣአምር፡ ከመ፡ ይጸንዕ፡ ህላዌ፡ ግፍዕ፡ ዲበ፡ ምድር፡ ወይትፌጸም፡ መቅሠፍት፡ ዐቢይ፡ ዲበ፡ ምድር፡ ወትትፌጸም፡ ኵላ፡ ዐመፃ፡ ወትትገዘም፡ እምሥረዊሃ፡ ወኵሉ፡ ሕንጻ፡ የኀልፍ።**

*ʾəsəmä ʾaʾämərə kämä yəṣänəʿə həlawe gəfəʿə dibä mədərə wäyətəfeṣämə mäqəśäfətə ʿäbiyə dibä mədərə wätətəfeṣämə kwəla ʿämäṣ́a wätətəgäzämə ʾəməśəräwiha wäkwəlu ḥənəṣa yäḫäləfə*

**For I know that the (being | existence | condition) of (violence | oppression | wrong) grows strong upon the earth, and a great (chastisement | scourging | calamity) shall be brought to completion upon the earth, and all (iniquity | injustice | lawlessness) shall be brought to completion and shall be cut off from its roots, and every (building | structure | edifice) shall pass away.**

- ⭐ Dillmann cites **this verse three separate times** — under `ግፍዕ` (*oppressio, vis, violentia, injuria*, "Hen. 91, 5"), under `ዐመፃ` (*injustitia, iniquitas, improbitas*, "Hen. 91, 5. 6"), and under `ሕንጻ` (*aedificatio, aedificium*, quoting `ዙሉ፡ሕንጻ፣ Hen. 91, 5`). The 1865 lexicon treats 91:5 as a defining occurrence of all three nouns.
- `ይትፌጸም` *yətəfeṣämə* is used twice in one verse with opposite force: the chastisement is "completed" (brought about) and the iniquity is "completed" (brought to an end). Ge'ez does not mark the difference; English cannot help marking it.
- `ህላዌ` *həlawe* — (being | existence | state | condition | substance).

### 91:6

**ወትደግም፡ ካዕበ፡ ዐመፃ፡ ወትትፌጸም፡ ዲበ፡ ምድር፡ ወትትአኀዝ፡ ኵላ፡ ግብረ፡ ዐመፃ፡ ወግብረ፡ ግፍዕ፡ ወአበሳ፡ ካዕበተ።**

*wätədägəmə kaʿəbä ʿämäṣ́a wätətəfeṣämə dibä mədərə wätətəʾäḫäzə kwəla gəbərä ʿämäṣ́a wägəbərä gəfəʿə wäʾäbäsa kaʿəbätä*

**And (iniquity | injustice) shall return a second time and be brought to completion upon the earth, and every work of (iniquity | injustice) and work of (violence | oppression) and (transgression | offence) shall be (seized | taken hold of | begun | prevail) a second time.**

- ⚠️ `ትትአኀዝ` *tətəʾäḫäzə* — passive/reflexive of `አኀዘ` (to seize, to hold, to begin). "Shall be seized", "shall take hold", "shall be begun" are all live. **I could not settle it.** The verse is about a *recurrence*, which suits "begin again", but the passive suits "be laid hold of (for judgement)". Printed unresolved.
- `ካዕበተ` twice in one verse — "a second time / again / doubly".

### 91:7

**ወእመኒ፡ ትልህቅ፡ ዐመፃ፡ ወኀጢአት፡ ወፅርፈት፡ ወግፍዕ፡ ወኵሉ፡ ግብር፡ ወትልህቅ፡ ዕልወት፡ ወአበሳ፡ ወርኵስ፡ መቅሠፍት፡ ዐቢይ፡ ይከውን፡ እምሰማይ፡ ዲበ፡ እሉ፡ ኵሎሙ፡ ወይወፅእ፡ እግዚእ፡ ቅዱስ፡ በመዓት፡ ወመቅሠፍት፡ ዐቢይ፡ ከመ፡ ይግበር፡ ኵነኔ፡ ዲበ፡ ምድር።**

*wäʾəmäni tələhəqə ʿämäṣ́a wäḫäṭiʾätə wäṣ́ərəfätə wägəfəʿə wäkwəlu gəbərə wätələhəqə ʿələwätə wäʾäbäsa wärəkwəsə mäqəśäfətə ʿäbiyə yəkäwənə ʾəməsämayə dibä ʾəlu kwəlomu wäyəwäṣ́əʾə ʾəgəziʾə qədusə bämäʿatə wämäqəśäfətə ʿäbiyə kämä yəgəbärə kwənäne dibä mədərə*

**And when (iniquity | injustice) and sin and (blasphemy | reviling) and (violence | oppression) and every deed grow great, and (apostasy | perversion | rebellion) and (transgression | offence) and (uncleanness | defilement) grow great, a great (chastisement | calamity) shall come from heaven upon all of these; and the holy Lord shall go forth in wrath and great (chastisement | calamity) to execute (judgement | condemnation | punishment) upon the earth.**

- `ፅርፈት` *ḍərəfätə* — (blasphemy | reviling | insult). Paired here with `ዕልወት` *ʿələwätə* (apostasy | turning aside | perversion | rebellion) and `ርኵስ` *rəkwəsə* (uncleanness | pollution).
- `እግዚእ፡ ቅዱስ` — "the holy Lord", the standing title in the Epistle.

### 91:8

**ወበእማንቱ፡ መዋዕል፡ ትትገዘም፡ ግፍዕ፡ እምነ፡ ሥረዊሃ፡ ወአሥራወ፡ ዐመፃ፡ ምስለ፡ ጕሕሉት፡ ወይትሀጐሉ፡ እምታሕተ፡ ሰማይ።**

*wäbäʾəmanətu mäwaʿələ tətəgäzämə gəfəʿə ʾəmənä śəräwiha wäʾäśərawä ʿämäṣ́a məsəlä gwəḥəlutə wäyətəhägwälu ʾəmətaḥətä sämayə*

**And in those days (violence | oppression) shall be cut off from its roots, and the roots of (iniquity | injustice) together with (deceit | fraud | craft), and they shall be destroyed from under heaven.**

- `ጕሕሉት` *gwəḥəlutə* — (deceit | guile | fraud | cunning).
- The root-cutting image is repeated from 91:5 and again at 91:11; three times in eleven verses.

### 91:9

**ወኵሉ፡ ይትወሀብ፡ ምስለ፡ አሕዛብ፡ ወማኅፈድ፡ በእሳት፡ ትነድድ፡ ወያወፅእዎሙ፡ እምኵሉ፡ ምድር፡ ወይትገደፉ፡ በኵነኔ፡ እሳት፡ ወይትሀጐሉ፡ በመዓት፡ ወበኵነኔ፡ ኀያል፡ እንተ፡ ለዓለም።**

*wäkwəlu yətəwähäbə məsəlä ʾäḥəzabə wämaḫəfädə bäʾəsatə tənädədə wäyawäṣ́əʾəwomu ʾəməkwəlu mədərə wäyətəgädäfu bäkwənäne ʾəsatə wäyətəhägwälu bämäʿatə wäbäkwənäne ḫäyalə ʾənətä läʿalämə*

**And all shall be given over together with the (nations | peoples | gentiles), and the tower shall burn with fire, and they shall drive them out from all the earth, and they shall be cast into the (judgement | condemnation | punishment) of fire, and they shall perish in wrath and in a mighty (judgement | condemnation) that is forever.**

- ⭐ `ማኅፈድ` *maḫəfädə*, "tower / fortress / stronghold". **Concordance fact, from the corpus only:** this noun occurs seven times in all of 1 Enoch, and every other occurrence is in the Animal Apocalypse — **89:50, 89:66, 89:67, 89:73** — where it is the structure the sheep build and which is burnt. So the Epistle's single use of the word lands on a term the book has already loaded elsewhere. 🛑 I am reporting the concordance, **not** identifying the two towers. Nothing in chapter 91 says what the tower is.
- `አሕዛብ` *ʾäḥəzabə* — (nations | peoples | gentiles | heathen). Ge'ez does not distinguish the ethnic from the religious sense.
- ⚠️ The referents drift: "all shall be given over", "**they** shall drive **them** out", "they shall be cast". Ge'ez leaves every one of these unspecified. I have not supplied subjects the text does not have.

### 91:10

**ወይትነሣእ፡ ጻድቅ፡ እምንዋም፡ ወይትነሣእ፡ ጥበብ፡ ወይትወሀብ፡ ሎሙ።**

*wäyətənäśaʾə ṣadəqə ʾəmənəwamə wäyətənäśaʾə ṭəbäbə wäyətəwähäbə lomu*

**And the (righteous one | just one | upright one) shall arise from sleep, and wisdom shall arise, and it shall be given to them.**

- `ጻድቅ` is singular and may be collective; "the righteous" as a class or "the righteous man". Unmarked in Ge'ez.
- ⭐ The same sentence is repeated almost verbatim at **92:3** — `ወይትነሣእ፡ ጻድቅ፡ እምንዋም` — inside the *other* superscription. The doublet is in the Ge'ez; I have not harmonised the two renderings, and the small differences between them are printed where they occur.
- `ንዋም` *nəwamə* — (sleep | slumber). Whether this is death or torpor the Ge'ez does not say.

### 91:11

**ወእምኔሁ፡ ይትገዘሙ፡ አሥራወ፡ ዐመፃ፡ ወኃጥኣን፡ ይትሀጐሉ፡ በሰይፍ፡ ወእምነ፡ ፅሩፋን፡ ይትገዘሙ፡ በኵሉ፡ መካን፡ ወእለ፡ ይኄልይዋ፡ ለግፍዕ፡ ወእለ፡ ይገብርዋ፡ ለፅርፈት፡ ይትሀጐሉ፡ በመጥባሕት።**

*wäʾəmənehu yətəgäzämu ʾäśərawä ʿämäṣ́a wäḫaṭəʾanə yətəhägwälu bäsäyəfə wäʾəmənä ṣ́ərufanə yətəgäzämu bäkwəlu mäkanə wäʾəlä yəḫeləyəwa lägəfəʿə wäʾəlä yəgäbərəwa läṣ́ərəfätə yətəhägwälu bämäṭəbaḥətə*

🛑 **THE SEAM. From here to 91:17 the Ge'ez narrates weeks EIGHT, NINE and TEN — before weeks one through seven have been given at 93:1–10. See the header. Nothing has been moved.**

**And thereafter the roots of (iniquity | injustice) shall be cut off, and the sinners shall perish by the sword; and from the (blasphemers | revilers) they shall be cut off in every place; and those who (devise | plot | intend) (violence | oppression) and those who commit (blasphemy | reviling) shall perish by the (slaughtering-knife | butcher's blade).**

- ⚠️ `ወእምነ፡ ፅሩፋን፡ ይትገዘሙ` — "and from the blasphemers they shall be cut off". The syntax is awkward in the Ge'ez as it stands: an ablative `እምነ` with a passive verb and no expressed subject. **Printed as it stands, not repaired.**
- `መጥባሕት` *mäṭəbaḥətə* — the noun of `ጠብሐ`, to slaughter (an animal). Not the `ሰይፍ` (sword) of the first clause. The verse uses two different blades.

### 91:12

**ወእምድኅረ፡ ዝ፡ ትከውን፡ ካልእት፡ ሰንበት፡ ሳምኒት፡ እንተ፡ ጽድቅ፡ ወይትወሀብ፡ ላቲ፡ ሰይፍ፡ ከመ፡ ይትገበር፡ ኵነኔ፡ ጽድቅ፡ እምእለ፡ ይገፍዑ፡ ወይትመጠዉ፡ ኃጥኣን፡ ለጻድቃን።**

*wäʾəmədəḫərä zə təkäwənə kaləʾətə sänəbätə samənitə ʾənətä ṣədəqə wäyətəwähäbə lati säyəfə kämä yətəgäbärə kwənäne ṣədəqə ʾəməʾəlä yəgäfəʿu wäyətəmäṭäwu ḫaṭəʾanə läṣadəqanə*

**And after this there shall be another week — an eighth — that of (righteousness | justice), and a sword shall be given to it, that (judgement | condemnation) of (righteousness | justice) may be executed upon the oppressors; and sinners shall be handed over into the hands of the righteous.**

- ⭐ `ካልእት፡ ሰንበት፡ ሳምኒት` — literally "**another** sabbath, an eighth". The word `ካልእት` presupposes a preceding member of the series. **In the Ge'ez as we have it, there is none**: 91:11 spoke of roots and swords, not of a seventh week. This is one of the two internal seams named in the header, and it is visible without appealing to any other manuscript.
- `ሰንበት` — sabbath **and** week, one word. See the vocabulary table; the vision counts sabbaths.
- `ይትመጠዉ` *yətəmäṭäwu* — (be handed over | be delivered up | be given into the hand of).

### 91:13

**በተፍጻሜታ፡ ያጠርዩ፡ አብያተ፡ እምጽድቀ፡ ዚኣሆሙ፡ ወይትሐነጽ፡ ቤት፡ ለንጉሥ፡ ዐቢይ፡ በስብሐት፡ እስከ፡ ለዓለም።**

*bätäfəṣameta yaṭärəyu ʾäbəyatä ʾəməṣədəqä ziʾahomu wäyətəḥänäṣə betə länəguśə ʿäbiyə bäsəbəḥätə ʾəsəkä läʿalämə*

**At its completion they shall acquire houses by their own (righteousness | justice | almsgiving), and a house shall be built for the great King in glory, unto eternity.**

- ⚠️ ⭐ `እምጽድቀ፡ ዚኣሆሙ` — "out of / by their own `ጽድቅ`". Dillmann's range for `ጽድቅ` includes ***beneficentia, misericordia, eleemosyna*** — almsgiving. So "they shall acquire houses **by their almsgiving**" is inside the word's attested range and not a strained reading, given that the clause is about *acquiring property*. I flag it and decline to choose; the sense-set is printed in full above and applies here.
- Two `ቤት`/`አብያት` in one verse: the houses they get and the house that is built. The Ge'ez uses the same noun for both.

### 91:14

**ወእምድኅረ፡ ዝ፡ በሰንበት፡ ታስዕት፡ ባቲ፡ ኵነኔ፡ ጽድቅ፡ ትትከሠት፡ ለኵሉ፡ ዓለም፡ ወኵሉ፡ ተግባረ፡ ረሲዓን፡ ይወፅእ፡ እምዲበ፡ ምድር፡ ወይጸሐፍ፡ ዓለም፡ ለሀጕል፡ ወኵሉ፡ ሰብእ፡ ይኔጽሩ፡ ለፍኖተ፡ ርትዕ።**

*wäʾəmədəḫərä zə bäsänəbätə tasəʿətə bati kwənäne ṣədəqə tətəkäśätə läkwəlu ʿalämə wäkwəlu tägəbarä räsiʿanə yəwäṣ́əʾə ʾəmədibä mədərə wäyəṣäḥäfə ʿalämə lähägwələ wäkwəlu säbəʾə yəneṣəru läfənotä rətəʿə*

**And after this, in the ninth week, the (judgement | condemnation) of (righteousness | justice) shall be revealed to the whole world, and every work of the (wicked | impious) shall depart from off the earth, and the world shall be written down for (destruction | ruin | perdition), and all men shall look toward the way of (uprightness | truth).**

- `ይጸሐፍ፡ ዓለም፡ ለሀጕል` — "the world shall be **written** for destruction". The verb is the ordinary verb of writing; the image is a register or a deed, not a decree spoken.
- `ረሲዓን` *räsiʿanə* — Dillmann glosses `ረሲዕ` as *impius, injustus*: (impious | wicked | unjust | godless).

### 91:15

**ወእምድኅረ፡ ዝንቱ፡ በሰንበት፡ ዐሥርት፡ ሳብዓቱ፡ እድ፡ ባቲ፡ ኵነኔ፡ እንተ፡ ለዓለም፡ ወትትገበር፡ እምትጉሃን፡ ወሰማይ፡ ዘለዓለም፡ ዐቢይ፡ ዘይበቊል፡ እማእከሎሙ፡ ለመላእክት።**

*wäʾəmədəḫərä zənətu bäsänəbätə ʿäśərətə sabəʿatu ʾədə bati kwənäne ʾənətä läʿalämə wätətəgäbärə ʾəmətəguhanə wäsämayə zäläʿalämə ʿäbiyə zäyəbäqwilə ʾəmaʾəkälomu lämälaʾəkətə*

**And after this, in the tenth week — its seventh part — there is the (judgement | condemnation) that is forever, and it shall be executed upon the (Watchers | Wakeful Ones), and a great eternal heaven that (springs up | germinates | shoots forth) from the midst of the angels.**

- ⭐⭐ `ሳብዓቱ፡ እድ` — "its **seventh** `እድ`". `እድ` is the ordinary word for *hand*, which is why this phrase looks impossible. **Resolved from the corpus alone, with no outside help:** `እድ` occurs six times in the whole of 1 Enoch, and the other five are all in the Astronomical Book — **73:5, 73:6, 73:7, 74:3, 78:7** — where it is the technical term for a **fraction of the lunar disc**, the moon's light being reckoned in fourteen of them (`ዐሠርቱ፡ ወአርባዕቱ፡ እደ፡ ብርሃኑ`, 73:5). So `እድ` in this book means a *fractional part*, and 91:15 says the seventh part of the tenth week. The Apocalypse of Weeks here reaches for the vocabulary of the lunar calendar.
- ⭐ `ትጉሃን` *təguhanə* — Dillmann's first sense for `ትጉህ` is *vigil, pervigil*, and he marks it explicitly as **the epithet of angels** (ἐγρήγοροι), citing Hen. 1:5, 10:7, 10:15, 12:2–3, 13:10, 14:1, 15:2. The second sense is ordinary: *vigilanter attentus, sedulus, studiosus, diligens*. So the word is simultaneously a title and an adjective — (the Watchers | the Wakeful | the vigilant ones).
- ⚠️ The last clause has no verb of its own: "and a great eternal heaven which springs up from the midst of the angels" hangs off the sentence. **Left hanging**, as the Ge'ez leaves it.
- `ይበቊል` *yəbäqwələ* — (sprouts | germinates | grows up | springs forth). A plant verb applied to a heaven.

### 91:16

**ወሰማይ፡ ቀዳማይ፡ ይወፅእ፡ ወየኀልፍ፡ ወሰማይ፡ ሐዲስ፡ ይትረአይ፡ ወኵሉ፡ ኀይላተ፡ ሰማያት፡ ያበርሁ፡ ለዓለም፡ ሰብዐተ፡ ምክዕቢተ።**

*wäsämayə qädamayə yəwäṣ́əʾə wäyäḫäləfə wäsämayə ḥädisə yətəräʾäyə wäkwəlu ḫäyəlatä sämayatə yabärəhu läʿalämə säbəʿätä məkəʿəbitä*

**And the first heaven shall depart and pass away, and a new heaven shall appear, and all the powers of the heavens shall give light sevenfold, forever.**

- ⭐ `ሰብዐተ፡ ምክዕቢተ` — "seven, doubly". Dillmann's entry for `ምክዕቢት` (*duplum, duplus, dupliciter, bis*) has a second sense: added to numerals it forms *-plex*, "-fold" — and he **cites this verse by name**, `Hen. 91, 16`, alongside `ሰብዐቱ ምክዕቢት` = *septuplus* at Hen. 93:10. The two ends of the dislocated vision use the identical construction.

### 91:17

**ወእምድኅረ፡ ዝ፡ ብዙኃን፡ ሰንበታት፡ እለ፡ አልቦሙ፡ ኍልቊ፡ ለዓለም፡ በኂሩት፡ ወበጽድቅ፡ ይከውኑ፡ ወኀጢአት፡ እምህየ፡ ኢትትበሃል፡ እስከ፡ ለዓለም።**

*wäʾəmədəḫərä zə bəzuḫanə sänəbätatə ʾəlä ʾäləbomu ḫwələqwi läʿalämə bäḫirutə wäbäṣədəqə yəkäwənu wäḫäṭiʾätə ʾəməhəyä ʾitətəbähalə ʾəsəkä läʿalämə*

**And after this there shall be many weeks, without number, forever, in (goodness | kindness | virtue) and in (righteousness | truth); and sin shall not be spoken of there again, unto eternity.**

- `ኢትትበሃል` — passive of "to say": sin will not be *said*, not merely not committed. The vision ends on speech.
- `ኂሩት` *ḫirutə* — (goodness | kindness | benevolence | virtue).

### 91:18

**ወይእዜኒ፡ እብለክሙ፡ ደቂቅየ፡ ወኣርእየክሙ፡ ፍናዋተ፡ ጽድቅ፡ ወፍናዋተ፡ ግፍዕ፡ ወኣርእየክሙ፡ ካዕበ፡ ከመ፡ ታእምሩ፡ ዘይመጽእ።**

*wäyəʾəzeni ʾəbəläkəmu däqiqəyä wäʾarəʾəyäkəmu fənawatä ṣədəqə wäfənawatä gəfəʿə wäʾarəʾəyäkəmu kaʿəbä kämä taʾəməru zäyəmäṣəʾə*

**And now I tell you, my children, and I show you the paths of (righteousness | truth) and the paths of (violence | oppression); and I will show you again, that you may know what is coming.**

### 91:19

**ወይእዜኒ፡ ስምዑኒ፡ ደቂቅየ፡ ወሑሩ፡ በፍናዋተ፡ ጽድቅ፡ ወኢትሑሩ፡ በፍናዋተ፡ ግፍዕ፡ እስመ፡ ይትሀጐሉ፡ ለዓለም፡ ኵሎሙ፡ እለ፡ የሐውሩ፡ በፍኖተ፡ ዐመፃ።**

*wäyəʾəzeni səməʿuni däqiqəyä wäḥuru bäfənawatä ṣədəqə wäʾitəḥuru bäfənawatä gəfəʿə ʾəsəmä yətəhägwälu läʿalämə kwəlomu ʾəlä yäḥäwəru bäfənotä ʿämäṣ́a*

**And now hear me, my children, and walk in the paths of (righteousness | truth), and do not walk in the paths of (violence | oppression); for all who walk in the way of (iniquity | injustice) shall perish forever.**

- The chapter closes by repeating 91:18 with the verbs turned to imperatives — a Ge'ez doubling that is in the text, not a scribal slip I have smoothed.

## 1 Enoch 92 — the second superscription

**Witnesses: Ge'ez alone.** No Greek, no Latin, no Aramaic, no Ethiopic apparatus for any verse of this chapter.

⚠️ This chapter interrupts. 91 ended with the two ways; 93 begins the Apocalypse of Weeks at week one. Between them stands a fresh title page — *"That which was written by Enoch"* — as though a new work were starting. Reported where it stands.

### 92:1

**ዘተጽሕፈ፡ እምሄኖክ፡ ጸሐፈ፡ ዝ፡ ኵሉ፡ ትምህርተ፡ ጥበብ፡ እምኵሉ፡ ሰብእ፡ ስቡሕ፡ ወመኰንነ፡ ኵሉ፡ ምድር፡ ለኵሎሙ፡ ውሉድየ፡ እለ፡ የኀድሩ፡ ዲበ፡ ምድር፡ ወለትውልድ፡ ደኃራውያን፡ እለ፡ ይገብሩ፡ ርትዐ፡ ወሰላመ።**

*zätäṣəḥəfä ʾəməhenokə ṣäḥäfä zə kwəlu təməhərətä ṭəbäbə ʾəməkwəlu säbəʾə səbuḥə wämäkwänənä kwəlu mədərə läkwəlomu wəludəyä ʾəlä yäḫädəru dibä mədərə wälätəwələdə däḫarawəyanə ʾəlä yəgäbəru rətəʿä wäsälamä*

**That which was written by Enoch. He wrote all this teaching of wisdom — (praised | glorified | renowned) above every man, and (ruler | governor | judge) of all the earth — for all my children who dwell upon the earth, and for the later generations who do (uprightness | what is right | truth) and peace.**

- 🛑 **The superscription changes person mid-sentence.** It opens in the third person about Enoch (`ዘተጽሕፈ፡ እምሄኖክ`, "that which was written by Enoch"; `ጸሐፈ`, "he wrote") and then says `ውሉድየ` — "**my** children". The seam is in the Ge'ez. I have not smoothed it to "his children".
- ⭐ `መኰንን` *mäkwänənə*, "ruler", is from the same root as `ኵነኔ`, "judgement" (√ኰነነ: *judicare* … *principatum gerere, dominari*). Dillmann puts both under one head. The man who is "ruler of all the earth" here and the "judgement" that fills the vision are one word in Ge'ez. English must split them.
- `ስቡሕ` *səbuḥə* — (praised | glorified | renowned | excellent). The comparative is the bare preposition `እም-`, "from/than all men".

### 92:2

**ኢትሕዘን፡ መንፈሰክሙ፡ በአዝማን፡ እስመ፡ መዋዕለ፡ ወሀበ፡ ቅዱስ፡ ወዐቢይ፡ ለኵሉ።**

*ʾitəḥəzänə mänəfäsäkəmu bäʾäzəmanə ʾəsəmä mäwaʿəlä wähäbä qədusə wäʿäbiyə läkwəlu*

**Let not your spirit be grieved because of the (times | ages | seasons); for the Holy and Great One has appointed days for all.**

- `ወሀበ` is simply "gave": "has **given** days to all". I have rendered it "appointed" nowhere in the translation line and kept "given"'s force in the note — the Ge'ez has no word for appointment here.
- `አዝማን` — plural of `ዘመን`: (times | ages | epochs | seasons).

### 92:3

**ወይትነሣእ፡ ጻድቅ፡ እምንዋም፡ ይትነሣእ፡ ወየኀልፍ፡ በፍኖተ፡ ጽድቅ፡ ወኵሉ፡ ፍኖቱ፡ ወምሕዋሪሁ፡ በኂሩት፡ ወበሣህል፡ ዘለዓለም።**

*wäyətənäśaʾə ṣadəqə ʾəmənəwamə yətənäśaʾə wäyäḫäləfə bäfənotä ṣədəqə wäkwəlu fənotu wäməḥəwarihu bäḫirutə wäbäśahələ zäläʿalämə*

**And the (righteous one | just one) shall arise from sleep; he shall arise, and shall pass along in the path of (righteousness | truth), and all his path and his (walking | course | journey) shall be in (goodness | kindness) and in (mercy | clemency | forbearance) forever.**

- ⭐ **Doublet of 91:10.** `ወይትነሣእ፡ ጻድቅ፡ እምንዋም` stands identically in both places — once at the end of the exhortation before the eighth week, once here inside the second superscription. The Ge'ez repeats it; **I have not harmonised my two renderings**, and the differences you see between this verse and 91:10 are differences the Ge'ez has (91:10 continues "and wisdom shall arise", this one continues "and shall pass along in the path").
- `ሣህል` *śahələ* — (mercy | pity | clemency | indulgence | forbearance).

### 92:4

**ይሠሀሎ፡ ለጻድቅ፡ ወሎቱ፡ ይሁብ፡ ርትዐ፡ ዘለዓለም፡ ወይሁብ፡ ሥልጣነ፡ ወይከውን፡ በኂሩት፡ ወበጽድቅ፡ ወየሐውር፡ ብርሃን፡ ዘለዓለም።**

*yəśähälo läṣadəqə wälotu yəhubə rətəʿä zäläʿalämə wäyəhubə śələṭanä wäyəkäwənə bäḫirutə wäbäṣədəqə wäyäḥäwərə bərəhanə zäläʿalämə*

**He shall have mercy on the (righteous one | just one), and to him he shall give (uprightness | what is right) forever, and he shall give (authority | power | dominion); and he shall be in (goodness | kindness) and in (righteousness | truth), and (he shall walk in | there shall go) eternal light.**

- ⚠️ `ወየሐውር፡ ብርሃን፡ ዘለዓለም` — **there is no preposition.** Read one way the subject is the man and `ብርሃን` is an adverbial accusative: "he shall walk in eternal light". Read the other way `ብርሃን` is the subject: "and eternal light shall go / shall walk". **The Ge'ez does not decide, and neither have I.**
- `ሥልጣን` *śələṭanə* — (authority | power | dominion | rule).

### 92:5

**ወኀጢአት፡ በጽልመት፡ ትትሀጐል፡ እስከ፡ ለዓለም፡ ወኢትትረአይ፡ እንከ፡ እምይእቲ፡ ዕለት፡ እስከ፡ ለዓለም።**

*wäḫäṭiʾätə bäṣələmätə tətəhägwälə ʾəsəkä läʿalämə wäʾitətəräʾäyə ʾənəkä ʾəməyəʾəti ʿəlätə ʾəsəkä läʿalämə*

**And sin shall (perish | be destroyed) in darkness forever, and shall not be seen again from that day unto eternity.**

- The chapter ends where 91:17 ended — sin removed from sight, sin removed from speech. Two different verbs, `ኢትትረአይ` (be seen) here and `ኢትትበሃል` (be spoken) there.

## 1 Enoch 93 — the Apocalypse of Weeks, weeks ONE to SEVEN · and the hymn

**Witnesses: Ge'ez alone.** No Greek, no Latin, no Aramaic, no Ethiopic apparatus for any verse of this chapter.

🛑 **This is where the vision begins — after its own ending.** Weeks eight, nine and ten stand at 91:12–17, above. The order below is the order the Ge'ez has.

### 93:1

**ወእምድኅረ፡ ዝ፡ ኮነ፡ ሄኖክ፡ ወአኀዘ፡ ይትናገር፡ እመጻሕፍት።**

*wäʾəmədəḫərä zə konä henokə wäʾäḫäzä yətənagärə ʾəmäṣaḥəfətə*

**And after this (it came to pass with | there was) Enoch, and he began to speak from the books.**

- ⚠️ `ኮነ፡ ሄኖክ` — "Enoch was / became / it came to pass". The verb has no complement. Ge'ez uses `ኮነ` as a narrative opener ("and it came to pass"), but here it takes Enoch as its subject and stops. **Printed bare.**
- `እመጻሕፍት` — "from the **books**", plural.

### 93:2

**ወይቤ፡ ሄኖክ፡ በእንተ፡ ውሉደ፡ ጽድቅ፡ ወበእንተ፡ ኅሩያነ፡ ዓለም፡ ወበእንተ፡ ተክለ፡ ጽድቅ፡ ወርትዕ፡ እሎንተ፡ እብለክሙ፡ ወኣየድዐክሙ፡ ደቂቅየ፡ አነ፡ ውእቱ፡ ሄኖክ፡ ዘአስተርአየኒ፡ እምራእየ፡ ሰማይ፡ ወእምቃለ፡ ቅዱሳን፡ መላእክት፡ አእመርኩ፡ ወእምጸፍጸፈ፡ ሰማይ፡ ለበውኩ።**

*wäyəbe henokə bäʾənətä wəludä ṣədəqə wäbäʾənətä ḫəruyanä ʿalämə wäbäʾənətä täkəlä ṣədəqə wärətəʿə ʾəlonətä ʾəbəläkəmu wäʾayädəʿäkəmu däqiqəyä ʾänä wəʾətu henokə zäʾäsətärəʾäyäni ʾəməraʾəyä sämayə wäʾəməqalä qədusanə mälaʾəkətə ʾäʾəmärəku wäʾəməṣäfəṣäfä sämayə läbäwəku*

**And Enoch spoke concerning the children of (righteousness | truth), and concerning the chosen of the world, and concerning the plant of (righteousness | truth) and (uprightness | what is right): these things I tell you and make known to you, my children — I, I am Enoch. From the vision of heaven it was shown to me, and from the word of the holy angels I learned, and from the (paving | tessellated floor | tablets) of heaven I understood.**

- ⭐⭐ `ጸፍጸፈ፡ ሰማይ` — **the heavenly tablets are, in Ge'ez, a mosaic pavement.** Dillmann's entry for `ጸፍጸፍ` gives two senses: 1) *pavimentum e lapidibus stratum, tessellatum* — a floor laid with stones, tessellated — citing **Hen. 14:10**; and 2) *tabulae vel tesserae, in coelo stratae* — tablets or tesserae spread out in heaven — for which the **only** authorities he cites are **Hen. 81:1, 81:2, 93:2, 103:2, 106:19**. That is every remaining occurrence of the word in this book and nothing outside it. So sense 2 exists in the 1865 lexicon *because of these five verses*, three of which fall in the present block. The image underneath is a floor of set stones that can be read.
- `ተክለ፡ ጽድቅ` — "the plant of righteousness". `ተክል` is the ordinary agricultural noun; the metaphor is a cutting or seedling, not a tree.
- `አነ፡ ውእቱ፡ ሄኖክ` — literally "I, he is Enoch", the Ge'ez emphatic identification.

### 93:3

**ወአኀዘ፡ እንከ፡ ይትናገር፡ ሄኖክ፡ እመጻሕፍት፡ ወይቤ፡ አነ፡ ሳብዕ፡ ተወለድኩ፡ በቀዳሚት፡ ሰንበት፡ እስከ፡ አመ፡ ኵነኔ፡ ጽድቅ፡ ተዐገሠ።**

*wäʾäḫäzä ʾənəkä yətənagärə henokə ʾəmäṣaḥəfətə wäyəbe ʾänä sabəʿə täwälädəku bäqädamitə sänəbätə ʾəsəkä ʾämä kwənäne ṣədəqə täʿägäśä*

**And Enoch then began to speak from the books, and said: I was born the seventh, in the first (sabbath | week), while (judgement | justice) and (righteousness | truth) still (endured | was patient | held back | waited).**

- 🛑 **WEEK ONE. The vision starts here — eighty-two verses after weeks eight, nine and ten were narrated at 91:12–17.** See the header. Nothing has been moved.
- ⚠️ `እስከ፡ አመ፡ ኵነኔ፡ ጽድቅ፡ ተዐገሠ` — "until when judgement/righteousness `ተዐገሠ`". The verb `ተዐገሠ` is (to endure | to be patient | to bear with | to wait | to hold out). Whether the sense is *judgement was still being patient* (i.e. had not yet fallen), or *judgement still held / lasted*, the Ge'ez does not settle. **Printed unresolved.**
- `ሳብዕ` — "seventh". Enoch is the seventh from Adam and is born in the first week; the vision's arithmetic starts on his own generation.

### 93:4

**ወትቀውም፡ እምድኅሬየ፡ በካልእት፡ ሰንበት፡ ዐባይ፡ እኪት፡ ወጕሕሉት፡ በቈለት፡ ወባቲ፡ ትከውን፡ ፍጻሜ፡ ቀዳሚት፡ ወባቲ፡ ይድኅን፡ ብእሲ፡ ወእምድኅረ፡ ተፈጸመ፡ ትልህቅ፡ ዐመፃ፡ ወሥርዐተ፡ ይገብር፡ ለኃጥኣን።**

*wätəqäwəmə ʾəmədəḫəreyä bäkaləʾətə sänəbätə ʿäbayə ʾəkitə wägwəḥəlutə bäqwälätə wäbati təkäwənə fəṣame qädamitə wäbati yədəḫənə bəʾəsi wäʾəmədəḫərä täfäṣämä tələhəqə ʿämäṣ́a wäśərəʿätä yəgäbərə läḫaṭəʾanə*

**And after me, in the second (sabbath | week), great (evil | wickedness) shall arise, and (deceit | guile | fraud) shall have sprouted; and in it there shall be the first (end | completion); and in it a man shall be (saved | delivered | escape); and after it is completed (iniquity | injustice) shall grow great, and he shall make an (ordinance | statute | order | law) for the sinners.**

- `በቈለት` *bäqwälätə* — the plant verb again (germinate | sprout | shoot up), used of deceit. The same verb is used of the new heaven at 91:15. Growth is this vision's controlling image, on both sides.
- ⚠️ `ወሥርዐተ፡ ይገብር፡ ለኃጥኣን` — the subject is unexpressed and the verb is 3ms: "**he** shall make an ordinance for the sinners". Who — God, or the man just saved, or iniquity personified — the Ge'ez does not say. **No subject supplied.**
- `ሥርዐት` *śərəʿätə* — (order | arrangement | statute | ordinance | law | rule).

### 93:5

**ወእምድኅረ፡ ዝ፡ በሣልስት፡ ሰንበት፡ በተፍጻሜታ፡ ይትኀረይ፡ ብእሲ፡ ለተክለ፡ ኵነኔ፡ ጽድቅ፡ ወእምድኅሬሁ፡ ይወፅእ፡ ተክለ፡ ጽድቅ፡ ወርትዕ፡ ለዓለም።**

*wäʾəmədəḫərä zə bäśaləsətə sänəbätə bätäfəṣameta yətəḫäräyə bəʾəsi lätäkəlä kwənäne ṣədəqə wäʾəmədəḫərehu yəwäṣ́əʾə täkəlä ṣədəqə wärətəʿə läʿalämə*

**And after this, in the third (sabbath | week), at its completion, a man shall be chosen as the plant of the (judgement | justice) of (righteousness | truth); and after him shall come forth the plant of (righteousness | truth) and (uprightness | what is right) forever.**

- `ይትኀረይ` — passive: "shall be chosen / elected". The same root as `ኅሩያን`, "the chosen", at 93:2 and 93:10.

### 93:6

**ወእምድኅረ፡ ዝ፡ በራብዕት፡ ሰንበት፡ በተፍጻሜታ፡ ራእያተ፡ ቅዱሳን፡ ወጻድቃን፡ ይትረአዩ፡ ወሥርዐት፡ ለትውልደ፡ ትውልድ፡ ወዐጸድ፡ ይትገበር፡ ሎሙ።**

*wäʾəmədəḫərä zə bärabəʿətə sänəbätə bätäfəṣameta raʾəyatä qədusanə wäṣadəqanə yətəräʾäyu wäśərəʿätə lätəwələdä təwələdə wäʿäṣädə yətəgäbärə lomu*

**And after this, in the fourth (sabbath | week), at its completion, visions of the holy and (righteous | just) shall be seen, and an (ordinance | statute | law) for generation upon generation, and an (enclosure | court | courtyard | farmstead) shall be made for them.**

- ⭐ `ዐጸድ` *ʿäṣädə*. **Concordance fact, from the corpus only:** this noun occurs seven times in all of 1 Enoch, and every other occurrence is in the Animal Apocalypse — **89:2, 89:3, 89:4, 89:5**. Together with `ማኅፈድ` at 91:9 (whose only other occurrences are 89:50, 89:66, 89:67, 89:73), that is **two rare architectural nouns which the Apocalypse of Weeks shares with the Animal Apocalypse and with no other part of the book.** 🛑 I report the distribution; I do not draw a conclusion from it. Two data points is a pattern, not a proof.
- Dillmann's range for `ዐጸድ`: *area quaevis circumsepta* — 1a) *area, impluvium, chors*; 1b) *vestibulum, atrium*, of the tabernacle, the temple, or a palace; 1c) *villa, vicus, pagus, tentoria nomadum, quaevis habitatio circumsepta*. The one constant is that something is **fenced round**. Whether it is a temple court or a walled hamlet is not decidable here.

### 93:7

**ወእምድኅረ፡ ዝ፡ በኀምስ፡ ሰንበት፡ በተፍጻሜታ፡ ቤተ፡ ስብሐት፡ ወመንግሥት፡ ይትሐነጽ፡ እስከ፡ ለዓለም።**

*wäʾəmədəḫərä zə bäḫäməsə sänəbätə bätäfəṣameta betä səbəḥätə wämänəgəśətə yətəḥänäṣə ʾəsəkä läʿalämə*

**And after this, in the fifth (sabbath | week), at its completion, a house of glory and of kingdom shall be built, forever.**

### 93:8

**ወእምድኅረ፡ ዝ፡ በሳድስት፡ ሰንበት፡ እለ፡ ይከውኑ፡ ውስቴታ፡ ጽሉላን፡ ኵሎሙ፡ ወይትረሳዕ፡ ልቦሙ፡ ለኵሎሙ፡ እምጥበብ፡ ወባቲ፡ የዐርግ፡ ብእሲ፡ ወበተፍጻሜታ፡ ይውዒ፡ ቤተ፡ መንግሥት፡ በእሳት፡ ወባቲ፡ ይዘረዉ፡ ኵሉ፡ ዘመደ፡ ሥርወ፡ ኅሩይ።**

*wäʾəmədəḫərä zə bäsadəsətə sänəbätə ʾəlä yəkäwənu wəsəteta ṣəlulanə kwəlomu wäyətəräsaʿə ləbomu läkwəlomu ʾəməṭəbäbə wäbati yäʿärəgə bəʾəsi wäbätäfəṣameta yəwəʿi betä mänəgəśətə bäʾəsatə wäbati yəzäräwu kwəlu zämädä śərəwä ḫəruyə*

**And after this, in the sixth (sabbath | week), all who are in it shall be (darkened | blinded | benighted), and the hearts of them all shall forget wisdom; and in it a man shall ascend; and at its completion the house of kingdom shall burn with fire; and in it the whole race of the chosen root shall be scattered.**

- ⭐ `ጽሉላን` — from `ተጸለለ`, which Dillmann glosses *obumbrari i. e. obscurari, obtenebrari* — to be overshadowed, hence darkened — and then, b) *caecum i. e. inscium esse*, to be blind, that is, ignorant. He cites `ተጸለለ፡ ልበሙ` ("their heart was darkened", Joh 12:40) and **Hen. 89** for this. So the word is one image, not two: shade → blindness → not knowing. The next clause ("shall forget wisdom") is the same thought continued, which is why the shading sense should not be discarded in favour of the medical one.
- ⚠️ `የዐርግ፡ ብእሲ` — "a man ascends". No object, no destination. **Left as it stands.**
- The burning house here and the burning tower at 91:9 use different nouns (`ቤት` / `ማኅፈድ`) and the same fire.

### 93:9

**ወእምድኅረ፡ ዝ፡ በሳብዕት፡ ሰንበት፡ ትትነሣእ፡ ትውልድ፡ ዕሉት፡ ወብዙኅ፡ ምግባራቲሃ፡ ወኵሉ፡ ምግባራቲሃ፡ ዕልወት።**

*wäʾəmədəḫərä zə bäsabəʿətə sänəbätə tətənäśaʾə təwələdə ʿəlutə wäbəzuḫə məgəbaratiha wäkwəlu məgəbaratiha ʿələwätə*

**And after this, in the seventh (sabbath | week), a (perverse | apostate | rebellious) generation shall arise, and many shall be its deeds, and all its deeds (are) (apostasy | perversion | rebellion).**

### 93:10

**ወበተፍጻሜታ፡ ይትዐሰዩ፡ ኅሩያነ፡ ጽድቅ፡ እምተክለ፡ ጽድቅ፡ ዘለዓለም፡ እለ፡ ይትወሀብ፡ ሎሙ፡ ሰብዐቱ፡ ምክዕቢታተ፡ ትምህርት፡ ለኵሉ፡ ፍጥረተ፡ ዚኣሁ።**

*wäbätäfəṣameta yətəʿäsäyu ḫəruyanä ṣədəqə ʾəmətäkəlä ṣədəqə zäläʿalämə ʾəlä yətəwähäbə lomu säbəʿätu məkəʿəbitatä təməhərətə läkwəlu fəṭərätä ziʾahu*

**And at its completion the chosen of (righteousness | truth) shall be (rewarded | recompensed | requited) from the eternal plant of (righteousness | truth), to whom shall be given sevenfold instruction concerning all his creation.**

- ⭐ `ሰብዐቱ፡ ምክዕቢታተ` — "seven doublings". Dillmann's entry for `ምክዕቢት` (*duplum · duplus · dupliciter, bifariam · secundo, iterum · bis*) has a second sense in which the word is added to a numeral to form *-plex*, and he gives **this verse by name** as his example of *septuplus*: `ሰብዐቱ ምክዕቢት ምሕርት፡ septuplus Hen. 93, 10`. He also records the plural form `ምክዕቢታት` as occurring at **Hen. 93:10** specifically. The identical construction closes the other half of the vision at **91:16** (`ሰብዐተ፡ ምክዕቢተ`), which Dillmann likewise cites. **The two dislocated halves end with the same idiom.** That is a fact about the Ge'ez, and I am not using it to argue for a reordering.

### 93:11

**እስመ፡ መኑ፡ ውእቱ፡ እምውሉደ፡ ሰብእ፡ ዘይክል፡ ሰሚዐ፡ ቃሉ፡ ለቅዱስ፡ ወኢይትሀወክ፡ ወመኑ፡ ዘይክል፡ ከመ፡ የኀሊ፡ ኅሊናሁ፡ ወመኑ፡ ዘይክል፡ ነጽሮቶ፡ ለሰማይ፡ ወኵሎ፡ ምግባራቲሃ።**

*ʾəsəmä mänu wəʾətu ʾəməwəludä säbəʾə zäyəkələ sämiʿä qalu läqədusə wäʾiyətəhäwäkə wämänu zäyəkələ kämä yäḫäli ḫəlinahu wämänu zäyəkələ näṣəroto läsämayə wäkwəlo məgəbaratiha*

🛑 **AND THERE IS NO EIGHTH WEEK HERE.** The count has run 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 7 and stops. What follows is a hymn of unanswerable questions. The eighth, ninth and tenth weeks were given at 91:12–17. This is the seam, printed where the Ge'ez puts it.

**For who is there among the children of men who is able to hear the voice of the Holy One and not be (shaken | troubled | disturbed)? And who is able to think his thought? And who is able to look upon heaven and all its works?**

- `ኢይትሀወክ` — (be shaken | be agitated | be thrown into confusion | be troubled).
- `የኀሊ፡ ኅሊናሁ` — figura etymologica: "to think his thinking". Ge'ez says it with the noun and verb of one root; English cannot without sounding contrived, so the doubling is reported here rather than forced into the line.

### 93:12

**ወምንት፡ ውእቱ፡ ዘይክል፡ አእምሮ፡ ግብረ፡ ሰማይ፡ ወከመ፡ ይርአይ፡ ነፍሶ፡ ወእመ፡ አኮ፡ መንፈሶ፡ ወይክል፡ ነጊረ፡ ወእመ፡ አኮ፡ ዐሪገ፡ ወይሬኢ፡ ኵሎ፡ አክናፊሆሙ፡ ወይኄልይዎሙ፡ ወእመ፡ አኮ፡ ይገብር፡ ከማሆሙ።**

*wämənətə wəʾətu zäyəkələ ʾäʾəməro gəbərä sämayə wäkämä yərəʾäyə näfəso wäʾəmä ʾäko mänəfäso wäyəkələ nägirä wäʾəmä ʾäko ʿärigä wäyəreʾi kwəlo ʾäkənafihomu wäyəḫeləyəwomu wäʾəmä ʾäko yəgäbərə kämahomu*

**And what is he who is able to know the work of heaven — and to see its soul, or if not, its spirit — and is able to tell? Or if not, to ascend and see all their (wings | edges | extremities) and consider them, or if not, to do as they do?**

- ⚠️ **This is the hardest verse in the chapter and I am not going to pretend otherwise.** The Ge'ez is a chain of `ወእመ፡ አኮ` ("and if not") clauses hung on a single `ዘይክል` ("who is able"), with the referents of "its soul", "its spirit" and "their wings" all unexpressed. My rendering keeps the chain and keeps the gaps.
- ⭐ `አክናፊሆሙ` — `ክንፍ` in Dillmann is 1) *ala*, a wing, of birds and of angels; 2) *pinna piscis*, a fin; 3) *ora* — an edge: a) of a garment, a hem or fringe; b) **of the earth, *extremum***, as in "the four `ክነፊሃ` of the earth" (Isa 11:12), and of valleys, for which he cites **Hen. 30:3**. So "their wings" and "their extremities" are the same Ge'ez word and both are live here. Given that the subject is the works of heaven, **neither can be excluded.**

### 93:13

**ወመኑ፡ ውእቱ፡ ኵሉ፡ ብእሲ፡ ዘይክል፡ አእምሮተ፡ እፎ፡ ውእቱ፡ ራሕባ፡ ወኑኃ፡ ለምድር፡ ወለመኑ፡ ተርእያ፡ አምጣነ፡ ኵሎሙ።**

*wämänu wəʾətu kwəlu bəʾəsi zäyəkələ ʾäʾəmərotä ʾəfo wəʾətu raḥəba wänuḫa lämədərə wälämänu tärəʾəya ʾäməṭanä kwəlomu*

**And who is there — any man at all — who is able to know what is the breadth and the length of the earth? And to whom has the measure of them all been shown?**

### 93:14

**ወእፎመ፡ ቦቱ፡ ኵሉ፡ ብእሲ፡ ዘይክል፡ አእምሮተ፡ ኑኃ፡ ለሰማይ፡ ውእቱ፡ ልዕልናሃ፡ ወዲበ፡ ምንት፡ ጸንዐት፡ ወሚ፡ መጠን፡ ውእቱ፡ ኍልቆሙ፡ ለከዋክብት፡ ወበአይቴ፡ የዐርፉ፡ ኵሎሙ፡ ብርሃናት።**

*wäʾəfomä botu kwəlu bəʾəsi zäyəkələ ʾäʾəmərotä nuḫa läsämayə wəʾətu ləʿələnaha wädibä mənətə ṣänəʿätə wämi mäṭänə wəʾətu ḫwələqomu läkäwakəbətə wäbäʾäyəte yäʿärəfu kwəlomu bərəhanatə*

**And how is there any man who is able to know the length of heaven, and what is its height, and upon what it is (established | made firm), and how great is the number of the stars, and where all the lights come to rest?**

- `ኑኅ` is used of both earth (93:13) and heaven here: (length | extent | height). The pair `ራሕብ`/`ኑኅ` is breadth/length; then heaven gets `ኑኅ` *and* `ልዕልና` (height), so the same noun cannot be doing the same work in both verses.
- `የዐርፉ` — (rest | come to rest | cease | settle). The chapter ends on the resting-place of the lights, which is the subject matter of the Astronomical Book.

## 1 Enoch 94 — the two paths, and the first Woes

**Witnesses: Ge'ez alone.**

### 94:1

**ወይእዜኒ፡ እብለክሙ፡ ደቂቅየ፡ አፍቅርዋ፡ ለጽድቅ፡ ወባቲ፡ ሑሩ፡ እስመ፡ ፍናዋተ፡ ጽድቅ፡ ይደሉ፡ ይትወከፍዎሙ፡ ወፍናዋተ፡ ዐመፃ፡ ፍጡነ፡ ይትሀጐሉ፡ ወየሐጽጹ።**

*wäyəʾəzeni ʾəbəläkəmu däqiqəyä ʾäfəqərəwa läṣədəqə wäbati ḥuru ʾəsəmä fənawatä ṣədəqə yədälu yətəwäkäfəwomu wäfənawatä ʿämäṣ́a fəṭunä yətəhägwälu wäyäḥäṣəṣu*

**And now I say to you, my children: love (righteousness | truth), and walk in her; for the paths of (righteousness | truth) are (worthy | fit) to be received, but the paths of (iniquity | injustice) shall swiftly (perish | be destroyed) and (dwindle | fail | come to nothing).**

- `ይደሉ` — Dillmann's usage citations give *dignus est* and *decet, oportet*: (is worthy | is fitting | ought). The same verb returns at 94:4 and 95:6.
- `ፍጡነ` *fəṭunä* — (swiftly | suddenly | quickly). This adverb governs the tempo of the whole Woe-section: 94:6, 94:7, 95:6.

### 94:2

**ወለሰብእ፡ እሙራን፡ እምትውልድ፡ ይትከሠቱ፡ ፍናዋተ፡ ግፍዕ፡ ወሞት፡ ወይርሕቁ፡ እምኔሆሙ፡ ወኢይተልውዎሙ።**

*wäläsäbəʾə ʾəmuranə ʾəmətəwələdə yətəkäśätu fənawatä gəfəʿə wämotə wäyərəḥəqu ʾəmənehomu wäʾiyətäləwəwomu*

**And to men (known | marked out | notable) from a generation, the paths of (violence | oppression) and of death shall be revealed, and they shall keep far from them and shall not follow them.**

- ⚠️ `ለሰብእ፡ እሙራን፡ እምትውልድ` — "to men known from a generation". `እሙር` is the passive participle of `አእመረ` (to know): (known | recognised | notable | famous). Whether `እምትውልድ` means "from [birth in a] generation", "from [among] a generation", or "from generation [to generation]" the Ge'ez does not mark. **Not decided.**

### 94:3

**ወይእዜኒ፡ ለክሙ፡ እብለክሙ፡ ለጻድቃን፡ ኢትሑሩ፡ በፍናዋተ፡ እኩይ፡ ወግፍዕ፡ ወኢ፡ በፍናዋተ፡ ሞት፡ ወኢትቅረቡ፡ ኀቤሆሙ፡ ከመ፡ ኢትትሀጐሉ።**

*wäyəʾəzeni läkəmu ʾəbəläkəmu läṣadəqanə ʾitəḥuru bäfənawatä ʾəkuyə wägəfəʿə wäʾi bäfənawatä motə wäʾitəqəräbu ḫäbehomu kämä ʾitətəhägwälu*

**And now to you I say — to the righteous: do not walk in the paths of (evil | wickedness) and of (violence | oppression), nor in the paths of death; and do not draw near to them, lest you be destroyed.**

### 94:4

**አላ፡ ፍቅዱ፡ ወኅረዩ፡ ለክሙ፡ ጽድቀ፡ ወሕይወተ፡ ኅሪተ፡ ወሑሩ፡ በፍናዋተ፡ ሰላም፡ ከመ፡ ትሕየዉ፡ ወትደለዉ።**

*ʾäla fəqədu wäḫəräyu läkəmu ṣədəqä wäḥəyəwätä ḫəritä wäḥuru bäfənawatä sälamə kämä təḥəyäwu wätədäläwu*

**But seek out and choose for yourselves (righteousness | truth) and a chosen life; and walk in the paths of peace, that you may live and be (worthy | fit).**

- `ሕይወተ፡ ኅሪተ` — "a chosen life", using the same root as `ኅሩያን`, the elect. The adjective is applied to the life, not to the people.

### 94:5

**ወትእኅዙ፡ በኅሊና፡ ልብክሙ፡ ወኢይደምሰስ፡ ነገርየ፡ እምልብክሙ፡ እስመ፡ ኣአምር፡ ከመ፡ ያመክርዎሙ፡ ኃጥኣን፡ ለሰብእ፡ ከመ፡ ይግበሩ፡ ጥበበ፡ እኩየ፡ ወኵሉ፡ መካን፡ ኢይትረከብ፡ ላቲ፡ ወኵሉ፡ መከራ፡ ኢየሐጽጽ።**

*wätəʾəḫəzu bäḫəlina ləbəkəmu wäʾiyədäməsäsə nägärəyä ʾəmələbəkəmu ʾəsəmä ʾaʾämərə kämä yamäkərəwomu ḫaṭəʾanə läsäbəʾə kämä yəgəbäru ṭəbäbä ʾəkuyä wäkwəlu mäkanə ʾiyətəräkäbə lati wäkwəlu mäkära ʾiyäḥäṣəṣə*

**And hold fast in the (thought | mind | reflection) of your heart, and let not my word be blotted out from your heart; for I know that sinners will (counsel | advise | persuade) men to work an evil wisdom, and no place shall be found for her, and no (testing | trial | temptation) shall be lessened.**

- ⭐ `መከራ` — Dillmann: 1) *exploratio, probatio, examinatio* (the assaying of gold and silver, Prov 27:21); 2) *experimentum*; 3) *tentatio* — and he cites **Hen. 94:5** under sense 3. So the sense range runs (assay, proving | trial | temptation) and the metallurgical image sits underneath. It is worth noting that 94:7, two verses on, is about gold and silver.
- `ጥበበ፡ እኩየ` — "an evil wisdom". The book's positive word for wisdom, taking an adjective that reverses it.
- ⚠️ `ወኵሉ፡ መካን፡ ኢይትረከብ፡ ላቲ` — literally "and every place shall not be found for her". Ge'ez negates the verb, not the quantifier, so "no place at all shall be found for her" is the sense; the referent of "her" (wisdom? evil wisdom?) is unexpressed. **Not supplied.**

### 94:6

**አሌ፡ ሎሙ፡ ለእለ፡ የሐንጽዋ፡ ለዐመፃ፡ ወለግፍዕ፡ ወይሣርርዋ፡ ለጕሕሉት፡ እስመ፡ ፍጡነ፡ ይትነሠቱ፡ ወአልቦሙ፡ ሰላም።**

*ʾäle lomu läʾəlä yäḥänəṣəwa läʿämäṣ́a wälägəfəʿə wäyəśarərəwa lägwəḥəlutə ʾəsəmä fəṭunä yətənäśätu wäʾäləbomu sälamə*

**Woe to those who build up (iniquity | injustice) and (violence | oppression), and who lay the foundation of (deceit | guile); for suddenly they shall be uprooted, and they shall have no peace.**

- `ይትነሠቱ` — (be torn up | be uprooted | be plucked out). A plant verb used of builders; the sentence puts building and uprooting in one breath.
- 🛑 **From here the text becomes a series of Woes.** They run, with interruptions, from 94:6 to 100:9. Where the Greek arrives at 97:6 it will be possible to check them; until then, Ge'ez alone.

### 94:7

**አሌ፡ ሎሙ፡ ለእለ፡ የሐንጹ፡ አብያቲሆሙ፡ በኀጢአት፡ እስመ፡ እምኵሉ፡ መሠረቶሙ፡ ይትነሠቱ፡ ወበሰይፍ፡ ይወድቁ፡ ወእለ፡ ያጠርይዋ፡ ለወርቅ፡ ወለብሩር፡ በኵነኔ፡ ፍጡነ፡ ይትሀጐሉ።**

*ʾäle lomu läʾəlä yäḥänəṣu ʾäbəyatihomu bäḫäṭiʾätə ʾəsəmä ʾəməkwəlu mäśärätomu yətənäśätu wäbäsäyəfə yəwädəqu wäʾəlä yaṭärəyəwa läwärəqə wäläbərurə bäkwənäne fəṭunä yətəhägwälu*

**Woe to those who build their houses with sin; for from their whole foundation they shall be uprooted, and by the sword they shall fall. And those who acquire gold and silver shall perish suddenly in (judgement | condemnation).**

### 94:8

**አሌ፡ ለክሙ፡ አብዕልት፡ እስመ፡ ዲበ፡ ብዕልክሙ፡ ተወከልክሙ፡ ወእምነ፡ ብዕልክሙ፡ ትወፅኡ፡ እስመ፡ ኢተዘከርክምዎ፡ ለልዑል፡ በዕለተ፡ ብዕልክሙ።**

*ʾäle läkəmu ʾäbəʿələtə ʾəsəmä dibä bəʿələkəmu täwäkäləkəmu wäʾəmənä bəʿələkəmu təwäṣ́əʾu ʾəsəmä ʾitäzäkärəkəməwo läləʿulə bäʿəlätä bəʿələkəmu*

**Woe to you, rich ones, for in your riches you have trusted; and from your riches you shall (go out | depart | be brought out) — because you did not remember the Most High in the days of your riches.**

- ⚠️ `ትወፅኡ` — "you shall go out". The verb is intransitive; whether the riches are being left behind, or the rich are being expelled *from* them, is not marked.

### 94:9

**ወገበርክምዎ፡ ለፅርፈት፡ ወለዐመፃ፡ ወድልዋነ፡ ኮንክሙ፡ ለዕለተ፡ ክዕወተ፡ ደም፡ ወለዕለተ፡ ጽልመት፡ ወለዕለተ፡ ኵነኔ፡ ዐባይ።**

*wägäbärəkəməwo läṣ́ərəfätə wäläʿämäṣ́a wädələwanä konəkəmu läʿəlätä kəʿəwätä dämə wäläʿəlätä ṣələmätə wäläʿəlätä kwənäne ʿäbayə*

**And you have committed (blasphemy | reviling) and (iniquity | injustice), and you have become (ready | prepared | fit) for the day of the pouring out of blood, and for the day of darkness, and for the day of the great (judgement | condemnation).**

- `ድልዋነ፡ ኮንክሙ` — "you have become ready". Same root as `ይደሉ` at 94:1 ("worthy / fitting"). The word that measured the paths of righteousness now measures the rich against the day of blood.

### 94:10

**ከመ፡ ዝ፡ አነ፡ እብል፡ ወኣየድዐክሙ፡ ከመ፡ ይገፈትዐክሙ፡ ዘፈጠረክሙ፡ ወዲበ፡ ድቀትክሙ፡ ከመ፡ ኢይከውን፡ ምሕረት፡ ወፈጣሪክሙ፡ ይትፌሣሕ፡ በሀጕልክሙ።**

*kämä zə ʾänä ʾəbələ wäʾayädəʿäkəmu kämä yəgäfätəʿäkəmu zäfäṭäräkəmu wädibä dəqätəkəmu kämä ʾiyəkäwənə məḥərätə wäfäṭarikəmu yətəfeśaḥə bähägwələkəmu*

**Thus I say and make known to you: that he who created you will (cast you down | thrust you away | reject you), and over your fall there shall be no mercy; and your Creator shall rejoice at your (destruction | perdition).**

- 🛑 The verse says what it says. `ይትፌሣሕ` is the ordinary verb of rejoicing, and `ፈጣሪክሙ` is "your Creator". I have not softened it, and there is no witness here to check it against.

### 94:11

**ወጻድቃነ፡ ዚኣክሙ፡ ይከውኑ፡ በእማንቱ፡ መዋዕል፡ ጽእለተ፡ ለኃጥኣን፡ ወለረሲዓን።**

*wäṣadəqanä ziʾakəmu yəkäwənu bäʾəmanətu mäwaʿələ ṣəʾəlätä läḫaṭəʾanə wäläräsiʿanə*

**And your righteous ones shall be, in those days, a (reproach | reviling | insult) to the sinners and to the (wicked | impious).**

- ⚠️ `ወጻድቃነ፡ ዚኣክሙ` — "the righteous of you / your righteous ones", addressed to the rich just condemned. `ጽእለት` is (reproach | reviling | contumely | insult) — so either the righteous become an object of the sinners' reviling, or a reproach against them. **The Ge'ez direction is not marked and I have not chosen one.**

## 1 Enoch 95 — "who will give me my eyes"

**Witnesses: Ge'ez alone.**

### 95:1

**መኑ፡ ይሁበኒ፡ አዕይንትየ፡ ከመ፡ ይኩና፡ ደመናተ፡ ሰማይ፡ ወእብኪ፡ ዲቤክሙ፡ ወእክዐው፡ አንብዕየ፡ ከመ፡ ደመና፡ ማይ፡ ወኣዕርፍ፡ እምሐዘነ፡ ልብየ።**

*mänu yəhubäni ʾäʿəyənətəyä kämä yəkuna dämänatä sämayə wäʾəbəki dibekəmu wäʾəkəʿäwə ʾänəbəʿəyä kämä dämäna mayə wäʾaʿərəfə ʾəməḥäzänä ləbəyä*

**Who will give me my eyes, that they may become clouds of heaven, and that I may weep over you, and pour out my tears like a cloud of water, and find rest from the sorrow of my heart?**

- `መኑ፡ ይሁበኒ` — "who will give me", the Ge'ez optative idiom: "would that…". Printed literally because the idiom is the point.
- The image is doubled: eyes that *become* clouds, tears poured out *like* a cloud of water. Ge'ez uses the same noun `ደመና` twice.

### 95:2

**መኑ፡ ወሀበክሙ፡ ከመ፡ ትግበሩ፡ ጽልአ፡ ወእከየ፡ ወይረክበክሙ፡ ለኃጥኣን፡ ኵነኔ።**

*mänu wähäbäkəmu kämä təgəbäru ṣələʾä wäʾəkäyä wäyəräkəbäkəmu läḫaṭəʾanə kwənäne*

**Who has (given | granted | permitted) you, that you should work hatred and (evil | wickedness)? Therefore (judgement | condemnation) shall find you, sinners.**

- ⚠️ `መኑ፡ ወሀበክሙ፡ ከመ፡ ትግበሩ` — "who gave you that you should do". Ge'ez has no separate verb "to permit" here; `ወሀበ` is simply "gave". Whether the question is *who authorised you* or *who enabled you* is not distinguished.

### 95:3

**ኢትፍርሁ፡ ጻድቃን፡ እምኃጥኣን፡ እስመ፡ ካዕበ፡ ያገብኦሙ፡ እግዚአብሔር፡ ውስተ፡ እዴክሙ፡ ከመ፡ ትግበሩ፡ ኵነኔ፡ ላዕሌሆሙ፡ በከመ፡ ፈቀድክሙ።**

*ʾitəfərəhu ṣadəqanə ʾəməḫaṭəʾanə ʾəsəmä kaʿəbä yagäbəʾomu ʾəgəziʾäbəḥerə wəsətä ʾədekəmu kämä təgəbäru kwənäne laʿəlehomu bäkämä fäqädəkəmu*

**Fear not, you righteous, because of the sinners; for again the Lord shall deliver them into your hand, that you may execute (judgement | condemnation | punishment) upon them according as you desire.**

- 🛑 `በከመ፡ ፈቀድክሙ` — "according as you have wished / desired / willed". The clause hands the measure of the judgement to the righteous themselves. Reported as it stands.

### 95:4

**አሌ፡ ለክሙ፡ እለ፡ ታወግዙ፡ ግዘታተ፡ ከመ፡ ኢትፍትሑ፡ ወፈውስ፡ ርሑቅ፡ እምኔክሙ፡ በእንተ፡ ኀጢአተ፡ ዚኣክሙ።**

*ʾäle läkəmu ʾəlä tawägəzu gəzätatä kämä ʾitəfətəḥu wäfäwəsə rəḥuqə ʾəmənekəmu bäʾənətä ḫäṭiʾätä ziʾakəmu*

**Woe to you who pronounce (curses | anathemas | bans) so that you cannot be (loosed | absolved | acquitted); and healing shall be far from you, because of your sins.**

- ⚠️ `እለ፡ ታወግዙ፡ ግዘታተ፡ ከመ፡ ኢትፍትሑ` — figura etymologica ("you ban bans"), then a purpose or result clause: "so that / in order that you not be loosed". `ፈትሐ` is the ordinary verb of untying and of legal release — (loose | untie | absolve | acquit | give judgement for). The relation between the cursing and the not-being-loosed is unmarked in the Ge'ez: whether they curse *in order to* escape release, or curse *with the result that* there is no release for them, cannot be settled from the words. **Printed unresolved.**

### 95:5

**አሌ፡ ለክሙ፡ እለ፡ ትፈድዩ፡ እኩየ፡ ለቢጽክሙ፡ እስመ፡ ትትፈደዩ፡ በከመ፡ ምግባሪክሙ።**

*ʾäle läkəmu ʾəlä təfädəyu ʾəkuyä läbiṣəkəmu ʾəsəmä tətəfädäyu bäkämä məgəbarikəmu*

**Woe to you who repay (evil | wickedness) to your neighbour; for you shall be repaid according to your works.**

### 95:6

**አሌ፡ ለክሙ፡ ለሰማዕተ፡ ሐሰት፡ ወእለ፡ ትደልውዋ፡ ለዐመፃ፡ እስመ፡ ፍጡነ፡ ትትሀጐሉ።**

*ʾäle läkəmu läsämaʿətä ḥäsätə wäʾəlä tədäləwəwa läʿämäṣ́a ʾəsəmä fəṭunä tətəhägwälu*

**Woe to you, witnesses of falsehood, and to those who (weigh out | make ready | reckon fit) (iniquity | injustice); for you shall swiftly perish.**

- ⚠️ ⭐ `ትደልውዋ፡ ለዐመፃ` — the verb is the `ደለወ` of 94:1 and 94:9. Its range covers *to weigh* (in a balance), *to be worthy / fitting*, and *to make ready*. All three give sense here and they are very different pictures: those who **weigh out** iniquity as a merchant weighs, those who **make iniquity ready**, those who **count iniquity worthy**. **The Ge'ez does not choose. Neither have I.** It stands directly beside "witnesses of falsehood", which favours a courtroom or a scale — but that is context, not lexicon, and I am not converting it into a decision.

### 95:7

**አሌ፡ ለክሙ፡ ለኃጥኣን፡ እስመ፡ ለጻድቃን፡ ትሰድድዎሙ፡ እስመ፡ አንትሙ፡ ትትሜጠዉ፡ ወትሰደዱ፡ እምዐመፃ፡ ወይጸንዕ፡ በላዕሌክሙ፡ አርዑተ፡ ዚኣሆሙ።**

*ʾäle läkəmu läḫaṭəʾanə ʾəsəmä läṣadəqanə təsädədəwomu ʾəsəmä ʾänətəmu tətəmeṭäwu wätəsädädu ʾəməʿämäṣ́a wäyəṣänəʿə bälaʿəlekəmu ʾärəʿutä ziʾahomu*

**Woe to you, sinners, because you persecute the righteous; for you yourselves shall be handed over and persecuted by (iniquity | injustice), and their yoke shall be heavy upon you.**

- ⚠️ `ወይጸንዕ፡ በላዕሌክሙ፡ አርዑተ፡ ዚኣሆሙ` — "and their own yoke shall be strong / heavy upon you". Whose yoke: the sinners' own, or the righteous'? The pronoun is third plural and both antecedents are in the verse. **Not decided.**
- `ትትሜጠዉ` — the passive of the verb used at 91:12, where sinners are handed over into the hands of the righteous. The Woe and the vision use the same verb.
