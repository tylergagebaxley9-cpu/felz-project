# Our translation

Translated from the Hebrew and Greek directly. No English translation was
consulted or adapted.

## What this is built from, and what it is not

**Used — grammatical analysis, which is fact, not translation:**

- the consonantal text (`hebrew-ot/morphhb`, CC BY)
- morphological parsing codes — `HVhc` = Hebrew verb, hiphil, infinitive construct
- root identification by Strong's number (Strong's is 1890, public domain)
- the Dead Sea Scrolls, Septuagint and Samaritan readings for the same verse

**Not used — anyone else's English:**

- no published translation, ancient or modern
- **not** the STEPBible/TAHOT English glosses that `tools/gloss.py` reports.
  That tool is useful for seeing how *other* translators have rendered a root,
  and it is honest about being their work. It is not the basis of anything here.

**The semantic ranges below are stated from the lexical evidence of the language
itself** — how the root behaves across its occurrences — not copied from a
dictionary or a gloss file.

## How to read an entry

Each word gets: the pointed Hebrew, the bare consonants, the parse, the root and
its range of meaning, and the reason for the choice made. Then the verse whole.
Where the witnesses disagree, every reading is translated, not just the one that
ended up in print.

## Honest scope

The Hebrew Old Testament is 306,785 words; the Greek New Testament 137,779.
Translating **444,564 words** this way is a career, not a session. This file grows
verse by verse and will never be complete. Every verse in it is done properly or
not at all — no filler, no silent gaps. Where something is uncertain it says so.

# Deuteronomy 32:8

## Word by word

**בְּהַנְחֵל** · consonants `בהנחל` · parse `HR/Vhc` — preposition בְּ + verb, hiphil, infinitive construct
> Root **נחל** (n-ḥ-l). The base sense is to take or hold as an allotted portion —
> property that comes to you by right and division rather than by purchase. The
> hiphil is causative: to *cause* to hold a portion, to apportion out.
> Range: *to give as a portion · to apportion · to allot · to cause to inherit.*
> With בְּ + infinitive construct this is a temporal clause: **"when [he] apportioned"**.

**עֶלְיוֹן** · `עליון` · `HAamsa` — adjective, masculine singular absolute
> From **עלה**, to go up. The adjective means *upper, higher, highest* — used
> literally of an upper gate or upper pool, and as a divine title.
> Range: *upper · uppermost · Most High.*
> Here a title, standing alone as the subject: **"Elyon" / "the Most High"**.

**גּוֹיִם** · `גוים` · `HNcmpa` — noun, common, masculine plural absolute
> **גוי**. A people considered as a political or ethnic body — a nation with a
> territory. Distinct from עם below, which leans toward kinship.
> Range: *nation · people (as a body) · gentiles.*
> **"nations"**

**בְּהַפְרִידוֹ** · `בהפריד/ו` · `HR/Vhc/Sp3ms` — preposition + verb, hiphil, infinitive construct + 3ms suffix
> Root **פרד** (p-r-d): to divide, part, separate — used of things being pulled
> apart from one another. Hiphil causative, with "he" as the suffix subject.
> Range: *to separate · to divide · to part · to scatter apart.*
> **"when he separated"**

**בְּנֵי** · `בני` · `HNcmpc` — noun, common, masculine plural **construct**
> **בן**: son; more broadly a member of a class or group ("sons of the prophets"
> = members of a prophetic guild). Construct state — it must bind to what follows.
> Range: *sons of · children of · descendants of · members of.*
> **"the sons of"**

**אָדָם** · `אדם` · `HNcmsa` — noun, common, masculine singular absolute
> **אדם**: humankind collectively, a human being; also the name Adam.
> Range: *humankind · a human · mankind · Adam.*
> Here collective, without the article: **"Adam" / "humankind"**.

**יַצֵּב** · `יצב` · `HVhi3ms` — verb, hiphil, imperfect 3rd masculine singular
> Root **נצב** (n-ṣ-b): to stand, be stationed, take a stand. Hiphil: to set up,
> station, fix in place — used of setting up a pillar or a boundary marker.
> Range: *to set up · to station · to fix · to establish.*
> **"he fixed" / "he set in place"**

**גְּבֻלֹת** · `גבלת` · `HNcfpc` — noun, common, feminine plural **construct**
> **גבול / גבולה**: a border or boundary; by extension the territory inside it.
> Range: *boundary · border · territory · limit.*
> **"the boundaries of"**

**עַמִּים** · `עמים` · `HNcmpa` — noun, common, masculine plural absolute
> **עם**: a people understood as kin — the word is related to the sense of
> paternal kindred. Where גוי is political, עם leans familial.
> Range: *people · peoples · kinsfolk.*
> **"the peoples"**

**לְמִסְפַּר** · `ל/מספר` · `HR/Ncmsc` — preposition לְ + noun, construct
> **מספר**, from **ספר** to count. A number, a tally, a reckoning.
> Range: *number · count · tally.* With לְ: according to, in proportion to.
> **"according to the number of"**

**בְּנֵי** · `בני` · `HNcmpc` — as above: **"the sons of"**

**יִשְׂרָאֵל** · `ישראל` · `HNp` — proper noun
> **"Israel"** — *in the Masoretic text.* This is the contested word. See below.

## The verse — Masoretic text (Leningrad Codex, 1008 CE)

> **When Elyon apportioned the nations,
> when he separated the sons of Adam,
> he fixed the boundaries of the peoples
> according to the number of the sons of Israel.**

## The verse — Dead Sea Scrolls (scroll 4Q37, c. 250 BCE – 68 CE)

The scroll's final phrase reads **בני אלוהים**, not בני ישראל.

**אֱלֹהִים** · `אלוהים` — noun, masculine plural in form
> **אלוה / אלהים**. Plural in form; takes singular agreement when used of the God
> of Israel, plural agreement when used of gods or divine beings generally.
> Range: *God · gods · divine beings · god-like ones.*
> Here plural and counted — "according to the *number* of" — so the plural is real:
> **"the sons of God" / "the divine beings"**.

> **When Elyon apportioned the nations,
> when he separated the sons of Adam,
> he fixed the boundaries of the peoples
> according to the number of the sons of God.**

## The verse — Septuagint (Greek, translation c. 250–100 BCE)

Final phrase `κατὰ ἀριθμὸν ἀγγέλων θεοῦ`.

**ἀγγέλων** — noun, genitive masculine plural of **ἄγγελος**
> Base sense **messenger** — a human courier or a divine one. The English "angel"
> is this word carried over untranslated, which narrows it.
> Range: *messengers · envoys · angels.*

> **When the Most High divided out the nations,
> when he scattered the sons of Adam,
> he set the boundaries of the peoples
> according to the number of the messengers of God.**

## What the three readings do

The Hebrew scroll and the Greek translation agree: the nations are apportioned
**according to the number of divine beings**. The Masoretic manuscript — 900 years
younger than the scroll — reads **the sons of Israel** instead.

Two independent witnesses against one, and the two older ones agree. The Greek was
not inventing; it was translating a Hebrew text that read אלוהים.

On the older reading the passage has Elyon distributing the nations among divine
sons, with YHWH receiving Jacob as his own share — which is exactly what verse 9
then says. The Masoretic reading removes the divine beings and puts Israel in
their place, which also flattens the distinction the poem draws between **Elyon**
doing the apportioning and **YHWH** receiving a portion.

**What this does not establish:** why. The manuscripts show the reading changed and
which direction. They cannot show intent. A scribe harmonising to a familiar phrase
and a scribe editing theology leave the same mark on the page.

# Deuteronomy 32:9

## Word by word

**כִּי** · `כי` — conjunction. *for · because · indeed · when.* Here causal: **"for"**.

**חֵלֶק** · `חלק` · noun masculine singular construct
> Root **חלק**, to divide smooth or apportion. A share received by division —
> the same conceptual world as נחל above.
> Range: *portion · share · allotment · tract.* **"the portion of"**

**יְהוָה** · `יהוה` — the divine name, four consonants.
> Left untransliterated here. The pronunciation is not recoverable from the
> consonantal text; the Masoretic vowel points on this word are borrowed from
> אֲדֹנָי as a reading instruction, not a record of its sound. **"YHWH"**

**עַמּוֹ** · `עמ/ו` — noun + 3ms suffix. **"his people"** (עם as kin, as above)

**יַעֲקֹב** · `יעקב` — proper noun. **"Jacob"**

**חֶבֶל** · `חבל` · noun masculine singular construct
> A **measuring cord** — and by extension the parcel of land measured out with it.
> The image is a surveyor's line. Range: *cord · measuring line · measured portion.*
> **"the measured portion of"**

**נַחֲלָתוֹ** · `נחלת/ו` — noun + 3ms suffix, from **נחל** again
> **"his inheritance"** — the same root that opened verse 8, closing the frame.

## The verse

> **For YHWH's portion is his people;
> Jacob is the cord of his inheritance.**

The vocabulary of verse 8 returns: apportioning (נחל), a share (חלק), a measured
parcel (חבל). Verse 9 is the receipt for the distribution verse 8 describes —
Elyon divides, and YHWH's allotted share is Jacob.
