# CHAPTER 9 — The Four Look Down

## ⭐⭐⭐ FINDING 1: The Ge'ez and the Greek do not name the same four angels

This is verse 1, the first line of the chapter, and the two witnesses give two different lists.

| | 1st | 2nd | 3rd | 4th |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Ge'ez** (base) | ሚካኤል *Mikaʾel* | ገብርኤል *Gäbərəʾel* | **ሱሩያል *Suruyal*** | ኡርያል *ʾUryal* |
| **Ge'ez** `p` `Bertalotto` | ሚካኤል | ገብርኤል | ሱርያል *Suryal* | **ኡርያን *ʾUryan*** |
| **Greek** `Gizeh` | Μιχαήλ | **Οὐριήλ** | **Ῥαφαήλ** | Γαβριήλ |

Three separate disagreements, all in one verse:

1. ⭐ **Greek has Ῥαφαήλ (Raphael). No Ge'ez witness does.** The Ge'ez third name is ሱሩያል/ሱርያል — a name that is not Raphael by any sound-change.
2. ⭐ **The order is different.** Ge'ez runs Michael–Gabriel–Suruyal–Uriel; Greek runs Michael–Uriel–Raphael–Gabriel. Only Michael is in the same slot.
3. ⚠️ **The Ge'ez manuscripts disagree with the Ge'ez base text on the fourth name**: base ኡርያል (*ʾUryal*, ending -*al*, the normal theophoric ־אל) against `p`/`Bertalotto` ኡርያን (*ʾUryan*, ending -*an*). A one-letter difference that removes the divine element from the name.

🛑 With the Aramaic gone, **there is no way to say which list is older.** In chapter 6 the Aramaic could arbitrate the name-list and did — it showed Syncellus preserving an older form than Gizeh. Here that instrument does not exist. Four names, two irreconcilable lists, and nothing to decide between them.

## ⭐⭐⭐ FINDING 2: Gizeh 9:4 contains an entire sentence that no Ge'ez witness has — and it tells the end of the story early

Attached to the end of Greek 9:4, after the doxology:

> `τότε ὁ ὕψιστος ἐκέλευσε τοῖς ἁγίοις ἀρχαγγέλοις, καὶ ἔδησαν τοὺς ἐξάρχους αὐτῶν καὶ ἔβαλον αὐτοὺς εἰς τὴν ἄβυσσον, ἕως τῆς κρίσεως.`
>
> "Then the Most High commanded the holy archangels, and they bound their chiefs and cast them into the abyss, until the judgement."

**The Ge'ez has nothing corresponding, in any manuscript.**

⭐ This is not a stray gloss. It is a **compressed summary of the whole of chapter 10** — the binding, the casting into the pit, the reservation until judgement — inserted into the middle of the archangels' prayer, *before they have finished asking*. In the Ge'ez the angels ask their question in 9:11 and it is still open when chapter 10 begins. In Gizeh the answer has already been given seven verses earlier, and 9:5–11 then continues as if it had not been.

🛑 Two possibilities and no third witness to choose: either the Ge'ez lost it, or Gizeh gained it. **Both are printed. Neither is preferred.** What can be said without conjecture is that in Gizeh the passage is *narratively incoherent* — the plea continues past its own answer — and in the Ge'ez it is not.

## ⭐⭐ FINDING 3: `ይሰክዩ` is a courtroom verb, and Dillmann cites this very chapter for it

The verb the souls of the dead perform in 9:3 and 9:10 is `ሰከየ` *säkäyä*. Dillmann gives two senses:

1. **confugere** — to flee for refuge to a place or a person; *precibus vel auxilium petendo adire alqm*, to approach someone by asking for prayers or aid.
2. **queri; incusare, criminari, reum facere, accusare, agere in alqm** — to complain; to charge, to accuse, to make someone a defendant, **to bring an action against**.

⭐ **Dillmann files 1 Enoch 9:3 under sense 2**, in the sub-entry for the verb construed with `ኀበ` — *"apud quam queruntur vel actionem instituunt"*, "before whom they complain **or institute an action**." The derivatives confirm the register: `ሰካዪ` = *actor, accusator* (plaintiff, prosecutor); `ስክየት` = *actio, accusatio* (a legal action, an indictment); `ምስካይ` = *refugium, asylum* (a city of refuge).

⭐⭐ So the dead in 9:3 and 9:10 are not merely crying. **They are filing suit.** They are the plaintiffs; the request in 9:3 is `አብኡ፡ ለነ፡ ፍትሐ፡ ኀበ፡ ልዑል` — "bring in our `ፍትሕ` before the Most High," where `ፍትሕ` is Dillmann's *actus judicandi* / *sententia judicis*, **the hearing of a case and the sentence handed down in it**. The whole scene is legal procedure: plaintiffs, a filed complaint, a superior court, a verdict sought.

⚠️ Gizeh's `ἐντυγχάνουσιν` covers "petition, plead, appeal to" and is compatible with this, but it does **not** carry the accusatory half of the Ge'ez range. The Ge'ez is the more forensic of the two texts. Both senses are printed at every occurrence below.

## ⭐ FINDING 4: The Ge'ez prayer changes person mid-sentence; the Greek does not

Ge'ez 9:4 opens in the **third** person — `ወይቤሉ፡ ለእግዚኦሙ፡ ለንጉሥ፡ እስመ፡ እግዚኦሙ፡ ለአጋእዝት` "and they said to their Lord, to the King: **for he is** the Lord of lords" — then switches to the **second** person in the same verse: `ወስምከ፡ ቅዱስ` "and **thy** name is holy."

Gizeh is second person from the first word: `Σὺ εἶ κύριος τῶν κυρίων`, "**Thou art** Lord of lords."

⚠️ Also in 9:4, three lexical splits inside the doxology:

| Ge'ez | Greek | Note |
|---|---|---|
| `ወንጉሦሙ፡ ለነገሥት` "their King of **kings**" | `βασιλεὺς τῶν **αἰώνων**` "King of the **ages**" | *kings* vs *ages* |
| `ወስቡሕ` "and **praised / glorified**" | `καὶ **μέγα**` "and **great**" | different word |
| — | `καὶ εὐλογητὸν` | Ge'ez has `ወቡሩክ` but **after** a repeated clause, not here |

## THE TEXT

### 9:1

**ወአሜሃ፡ ሐወጹ፡ ሚካኤል፡ ወገብርኤል፡ ወሱሩያል፡ ወኡርያል፡ እምሰማይ፡ ወርእዩ፡ ብዙኀ፡ ደመ፡ ዘይትከዐው፡ በዲበ፡ ምድር፡ ወኵሎ፡ ዐመፃ፡ ዘይትገበር፡ በዲበ፡ ምድር።**

*wäʾämeha ḥäwäṣu mikaʾelə wägäbərəʾelə wäsuruyalə wäʾurəyalə ʾəməsämayə wärəʾəyu bəzuḫä dämä zäyətəkäʿäwə bädibä mədərə wäkwəlo ʿämäṣ́a zäyətəgäbärə bädibä mədərə*

**And then Mikaʾel and Gabrəʾel and Suruyal and ʾUryal (looked down | looked out | peered in | gazed | inspected | visited) from heaven, and they saw much blood being poured out upon the earth, and all the (injustice | iniquity | wickedness | lawlessness) that was being done upon the earth.**

- `ሐወጹ` *ḥäwäṣu* — Dillmann gives two ranges: 1) *spectare, speculari, introspicere, prospicere, despectare, perspicere* — to look, to spy out, to look in, to look forward, **to look down on**, to look through; 2) *inspicere, visitare* — to inspect, to visit, and specially of God, *visitare aut benefaciendo aut puniendo* — **to visit either to do good or to punish**. ⭐ The second sense is the technical word for divine visitation in judgement. Both are live here and both are printed. Gizeh's `παρακύψαντες` ("having leaned over / stooped to look") selects only the first.
- `ዐመፃ` *ʿämäṣ́a* — Dillmann: *injustitia, iniquitas, improbitas*; renders both ἀδικία and **ἀνομία**. The moral and the legal senses are one word.

**Witnesses:**
- ⭐ **Names — see Finding 1.** `p` `Bertalotto` read ሱርያል and **ኡርያን**; Gizeh reads Μιχαὴλ καὶ Οὐριὴλ καὶ **Ῥαφαὴλ** καὶ Γαβριήλ.
- ⭐ **Gizeh has only ONE of the two things seen.** It ends at `αἷμα πολὺ ἐκχυννόμενον ἐπὶ τῆς γῆς` — much blood poured out — and **lacks the whole second clause** `ወኵሎ፡ ዐመፃ፡ ዘይትገበር፡ በዲበ፡ ምድር` ("and all the iniquity that was being done upon the earth"). A Ge'ez plus of eight words, or a Greek loss.
- Gizeh has two verbs of seeing (`παρακύψαντες` … `ἐθεάσαντο`) matching the Ge'ez two (`ሐወጹ` … `ወርእዩ`); it adds a resumptive `οὗτοι` ("these") with no Ge'ez counterpart.
- Ethiopic orthographic only: `p` ብዙኃ, ዘይትከዓው / `Bertalotto` ብዙኀ, ዘይትከዐው; both mss ዓመፃ. `p` `Bertalotto` also read **ዙይገበር** for base ዘይትገበር — a corruption of the relative-passive.
- Qumran Aramaic — **ABSENT** (version exists, does not attest).
- Latin Fragments — **ABSENT** (version exists, does not attest).

### 9:2

**ወይቤሉ፡ በበይናቲሆሙ፡ ቃለ፡ ጽራሐቲሆሙ፡ ዕራቃ፡ ጸርሐት፡ ምድር፡ እስከ፡ አንቀጸ፡ ሰማይ።**

*wäyəbelu bäbäyənatihomu qalä ṣəraḥätihomu ʿəraqa ṣärəḥätə mədərə ʾəsəkä ʾänəqäṣä sämayə*

**And they said one to another: "The voice of their (cries | outcries | shoutings) — (stripped bare | bereft | emptied of her inhabitants | left alone | made void), the earth has cried out as far as the gate of heaven."**

- ⭐ `ዕራቃ` *ʿəraqa* — Dillmann, `ዕራቅ`, an adjective used **only** with pronominal suffixes; here the suffix is 3rd fem. sing., agreeing with `ምድር` (earth, fem.). Senses: 1) *nudus* — naked; a) *merus, solus, inanis* — mere, alone, empty; **b) *orbus, destitutus incolis*** — bereaved, **destitute of inhabitants** — and ⭐ **Dillmann cites 1 Enoch here for exactly this sense**, alongside 67:2; 2) *vacuus, inanis* — void, empty; a) *manibus vacuis* — empty-handed; b) *re infecta* — with nothing accomplished. **All are printed. None is preferred.** The sense Dillmann assigns to this passage is "emptied of the people who lived on her."
- `ጸርሐት` *ṣärəḥätə* — 3rd fem. sing. perfect: **the earth herself** is the one who cries out.
- `አንቀጸ፡ ሰማይ` — singular **gate**; Gizeh has plural `πυλῶν`.

**Witnesses:**
- 🛑 **The Greek has a different sentence.** Gizeh: `καὶ εἶπαν πρὸς ἀλλήλους φωνὴ βοώντων ἐπὶ τῆς γῆς μέχρι πυλῶν τοῦ οὐρανοῦ` — "and they said to one another: a voice of those crying out **on** the earth, as far as the gates of heaven." In the Greek the earth is a **location** (`ἐπὶ τῆς γῆς`). In the Ge'ez the earth is the **subject of a finite verb** — she cries out — and she is additionally described as `ዕራቃ`, for which the Greek has **nothing at all**. The Greek plural `πυλῶν` against the Ge'ez singular `አንቀጸ` is a further split.
- Ethiopic `p` `Bertalotto` agree with the base throughout, differing only in punctuation (`ምድር።እስከ` for `ምድር፡ እስከ`) — ⚠️ but that punctuation moves the sentence break, joining "as far as the gate of heaven" to what follows rather than to what precedes.
- Qumran Aramaic — **ABSENT**. Latin Fragments — **ABSENT**.

### 9:3

**ወይእዜኒ፡ ኦ፡ ቅዱሳነ፡ ሰማይ፡ ለክሙ፡ ይሰክዩ፡ ነፍሳተ፡ ሰብእ፡ እንዘ፡ ይብሉ፡ አብኡ፡ ለነ፡ ፍትሐ፡ ኀበ፡ ልዑል።**

*wäyəʾəzeni ʾo qədusanä sämayə läkəmu yəsäkəyu näfəsatä säbəʾə ʾənəzä yəbəlu ʾäbəʾu länä fətəḥä ḫäbä ləʿulə*

**"And now, O holy ones of heaven, to you the souls of (humankind | men) (take refuge | make petition | make complaint | lay their charge | bring their suit), saying: 'Bring in for us (a judgement | a hearing | a sentence | a verdict | our right) before the Most High.'"**

- ⭐ `ይሰክዩ` — **see Finding 3.** Dillmann files this verse under *queri … accusare, agere in alqm*, the forensic sense, while sense 1 *confugere / precibus adire* remains in range. Both printed.
- ⭐ `ፍትሐ` *fətəḥä* — Dillmann `ፍትሕ`: 1a) *actus judicandi* — the act of judging, the trial itself; 1b) *sententia judicis* — the judge's sentence. **The word is both the hearing and the verdict**; English cannot hold both at once and no choice is made here.
- `ሰብእ` *säbəʾə* — collective humanity, not males specifically; the same ambiguity as Greek `ἀνθρώπων`.

**Witnesses:**
- ⚠️ **Word order differs inside the Ge'ez tradition.** Base: `ኦ፡ ቅዱሳነ፡ ሰማይ፡ ለክሙ፡ ይሰክዩ` ("O holy ones of heaven, to you they plead"). `p` `Bertalotto`: `ወይእዜኒ፡ ለክሙ፡ አቅዱሳነ፡ ሰማይ፡ ይሰክዩ` — the vocative particle is written **አ**-, fused to the noun (`አቅዱሳነ`), and the phrase order is inverted.
- ⭐ **Gizeh lacks the address to the holy ones entirely.** It reads only `ἐντυγχάνουσιν αἱ ψυχαὶ τῶν ἀνθρώπων λεγόντων· Εἰσαγάγετε τὴν κρίσιν ἡμῶν πρὸς τὸν ὕψιστον.` — no "and now", no "O holy ones of heaven", no "to you". In the Ge'ez the archangels are being addressed; in the Greek nobody is.
- Gizeh `τὴν κρίσιν **ἡμῶν**` ("**our** judgement") against Ge'ez `ፍትሐ` with no possessive but a separate dative `ለነ` ("for us"). Same information, different syntax.
- Qumran Aramaic — **ABSENT**. Latin Fragments — **ABSENT**.

### 9:4

**ወይቤሉ፡ ለእግዚኦሙ፡ ለንጉሥ፡ እስመ፡ እግዚኦሙ፡ ለአጋእዝት፡ ወአምላኮሙ፡ ለአማልክት፡ ወንጉሦሙ፡ ለነገሥት፡ ወመንበረ፡ ስብሐቲሁ፡ ውስተ፡ ኵሉ፡ ትውልድ፡ ዘለዓለም፡ ወስምከ፡ ቅዱስ፡ ወስቡሕ፡ ውስተ፡ ኵሉ፡ ትውልድ፡ ዓለም፡ ወቡሩክ፡ ወስቡሕ።**

*wäyəbelu läʾəgəziʾomu länəguśə ʾəsəmä ʾəgəziʾomu läʾägaʾəzətə wäʾäməlakomu läʾämaləkətə wänəguśomu länägäśətə wämänəbärä səbəḥätihu wəsətä kwəlu təwələdə zäläʿalämə wäsəməkä qədusə wäsəbuḥə wəsətä kwəlu təwələdə ʿalämə wäburukə wäsəbuḥə*

**And they said to their Lord, to the King: "For he is the Lord of lords and the God of gods and the King of kings; and the throne of his (glory | praise | majesty) is unto every generation of eternity; and thy name is holy and (praised | glorified | extolled) unto every generation of the age, and blessed and (praised | glorified | extolled)."**

- ⭐ **The person shifts mid-verse** — `እግዚኦሙ` … `ስብሐቲሁ` (3rd person, "**his**") giving way to `ወስምከ` (2nd person, "**thy**"). See Finding 4. This is not smoothed here.
- `አጋእዝት` *ʾägaʾəzətə* — plural of `እግዚእ`, "lords, masters."
- `ስቡሕ` *səbuḥə* — the passive participle of "to praise, to glorify"; it is **praised**, not inherently **great**. Gizeh's `μέγα` is a different predicate.
- ⚠️ `ትውልድ፡ ዘለዓለም` / `ትውልድ፡ ዓለም` — the base text has two different constructions in the two halves ("generation **of** eternity" with the relative `ዘ`, then "generation of the age" without); the manuscripts have `ትውልደ፡ ዓለም` in both. Retained as printed.

**Witnesses:**
- ⭐⭐⭐ **Gizeh appends an entire sentence absent from every Ge'ez witness** — the binding and the casting into the abyss. See Finding 2. Text: `τότε ὁ ὕψιστος ἐκέλευσε τοῖς ἁγίοις ἀρχαγγέλοις, καὶ ἔδησαν τοὺς ἐξάρχους αὐτῶν καὶ ἔβαλον αὐτοὺς εἰς τὴν ἄβυσσον, ἕως τῆς κρίσεως.`
- ⭐ **Gizeh is 2nd person from the start**: `Σὺ εἶ κύριος τῶν κυρίων`. The Ge'ez `እስመ` ("for, because") introducing a 3rd-person clause has no Greek counterpart.
- ⭐ `βασιλεὺς τῶν **αἰώνων**` (King of the **ages**) against Ge'ez `ወንጉሦሙ፡ ለነገሥት` (King of **kings**). A genuine split, not orthography.
- ⭐ Gizeh `τὸ ὄνομά σου τὸ ἅγιον καὶ **μέγα** καὶ εὐλογητὸν` — *holy and **great** and blessed*, three terms once. Ge'ez has *holy and praised* … *blessed and praised*, four terms in **two** clauses separated by a repeated "unto every generation." ⚠️ The Ge'ez repetition (`ውስተ፡ ኵሉ፡ ትውልድ` twice, `ወስቡሕ` twice) may be dittography, or the Greek may have collapsed a genuine doublet. **Not decided.**
- ⚠️ `p` `Bertalotto` read `ለእግዚኦሙ፡ **ንጉሥ**` — without the second `ለ`, making "their Lord the King" one title rather than two datives.
- `p` ለአጋዕዝት / `Bertalotto` ለአጋእዝት — orthographic.
- Qumran Aramaic — **ABSENT**. Latin Fragments — **ABSENT**.

### 9:5

**አንተ፡ ወገበርከ፡ ኵሎ፡ ወሥልጣነ፡ ኵሉ፡ ምስሌከ፡ ወኵሉ፡ ክሡት፡ በቅድሜከ፡ ወግሁድ፡ ወአንተ፡ ትሬኢ፡ ኵሎ፡ ወአልቦ፡ ዘይትከሀል፡ ይትኀባእ፡ እምኔከ።**

*ʾänətä wägäbärəkä kwəlo wäśələṭanä kwəlu məsəlekä wäkwəlu kəśutə bäqədəmekä wägəhudə wäʾänətä təreʾi kwəlo wäʾäləbo zäyətəkähälə yətəḫäbaʾə ʾəmənekä*

**"Thou — thou hast made all things, and the (authority | power | dominion | jurisdiction) over all is with thee; and everything is (open | uncovered | revealed) before thee and (manifest | plain | evident | known to all); and thou seest all, and there is nothing that is able to hide itself from thee."**

- `ግሁድ` *gəhudə* — Dillmann: *manifestus, apertus, illustris, evidens, omnibus notus et perspectus* — manifest, open, conspicuous, evident, **known and seen through by everyone**. The last phrase is the strong one and is not reducible to "manifest."
- `ሥልጣን` *śələṭanə* — authority, power, right of disposal; Gizeh renders `ἐξουσίαν`, the same range.
- `ዘይትከሀል` *zäyətəkähälə* — "which is possible / which can be"; the impersonal, not a modal of the hider.

**Witnesses:**
- ⚠️ **The last two clauses of the Ge'ez verse are not missing from the Greek — they have moved.** Gizeh 9:5 ends at `καὶ πάντα ἐνώπιόν σου φανερὰ καὶ ἀκάλυπτα`, and Gizeh **9:6** then **opens** with `καὶ πάντα σὺ ὁρᾷς` ("and thou seest all"). This is a verse-division difference, and it must not be reported as a Greek omission. 🛑 What **is** absent from Gizeh at either point is the final clause `ወአልቦ፡ ዘይትከሀል፡ ይትኀባእ፡ እምኔከ` — "and there is nothing able to hide itself from thee."
- ⚠️ `p` `Bertalotto` omit the `ወ` on `ወገበርከ` (reading `አንተ፡ ገበርከ`, "thou hast made"), read `ወሥልጣን` in the **nominative** for base accusative `ወሥልጣነ`, and omit `በ` from `በቅድሜከ`. Three small syntactic differences in one verse.
- `p` ዘይትከሃል / `Bertalotto` ዘይትከሀል; both mss ይትኃባእ for base ይትኀባእ — orthographic.
- Qumran Aramaic — **ABSENT**. Latin Fragments — **ABSENT**.

### 9:6

**ርኢኬ፡ ዘገብረ፡ አዛዝኤል፡ ዘከመ፡ መሀረ፡ ኵሎ፡ ዐመፃ፡ በዲበ፡ ምድር፡ ወአግሀደ፡ ኅቡኣተ፡ ዓለም፡ እለ፡ ይትገበራ፡ በሰማያት።**

*rəʾike zägäbərä ʾäzazəʾelə zäkämä mähärä kwəlo ʿämäṣ́a bädibä mədərə wäʾägəhädä ḫəbuʾatä ʿalämə ʾəlä yətəgäbära bäsämayatə*

**"See, then, what ʾAzazəʾel has done — how he has taught all (injustice | iniquity | wickedness | lawlessness) upon the earth, and has (made manifest | uncovered | published | disclosed) the (hidden things | secrets | mysteries) of the (age | world | eternity), which are wrought in the heavens."**

- ⚠️ `ርኢኬ` *rəʾike* — as written in `p` and the base, an **imperative** with the enclitic `ኬ`: "look, then!" `Bertalotto` reads `ርኢከ` — a **perfect, 2nd masc. sing.**: "thou hast seen." One vowel-letter separates a command to God from a statement about God. 🛑 Gizeh sides with neither exactly: `καὶ πάντα σὺ ὁρᾷς ἃ ἐποίησεν` — a **present indicative**, "and thou seest all things which…". **Three different verb forms in three witnesses. All printed.**
- `ዓለም` *ʿalämə* — Ge'ez does not separate *world* (spatial) from *age/eternity* (temporal); Greek `αἰῶνος` has the same double range. Both printed.
- `ኅቡኣተ` *ḫəbuʾatä* — hidden things; Gizeh `μυστήρια`, which narrows toward "mysteries, initiatory secrets."

**Witnesses:**
- 🛑 **`p` and `Bertalotto` carry an obvious dittography:** `ኅቡኣተ፡ ዓለም፡ እለ፡ ይትገበራ፡ **ዓለም፡ እለ፡ ይትገበራ፡** በሰማያት` — the three words "of the age which are wrought" are written **twice**. Recorded, not silently corrected.
- ⭐ **Gizeh has a clause the Ge'ez base lacks:** after `ἃ ἐπιτηδεύουσιν` it adds `καὶ **ἔγνωσαν ἄνθρωποι**` — "and **men have come to know** [them]." ⚠️ This may not be a pure plus: `p` and `Bertalotto` end the verse `… በሰማያት፡ **ወአምእረ፡ ሰብአታተ።**` — a clause the base text instead assigns to the opening of 9:7 in the form `ወአመረ፡ ስብዓታተ`. **The two traditions have a clause at the same seam, both built on a verb of knowing/making-known** (`አመረ` / `ἔγνωσαν`), and in `p` the object is spelled `ሰብአታተ`, one letter from `ሰብእ` "men." 🛑 Whether these are the same clause diverging or two different clauses at a coincidental join **cannot be settled without the Aramaic, and is not settled here.**
- ⭐ **Name split:** Ge'ez `አዛዝኤል` *ʾAzazəʾel* against Gizeh `Ἀζαήλ` *Azaēl* — a syllable shorter, with no reduplicated *z*.
- ⚠️ Gizeh reads the relative as `τὰ ἐν τῷ **οὐρανῷ**`, **singular** heaven, against Ge'ez `በሰማያት`, **plural** heavens.
- `p` መሐረ / `Bertalotto` መሀረ; `p` ወአግኃደ / `Bertalotto` ወአግሀደ; both mss ዓመፃ — orthographic.
- Qumran Aramaic — **ABSENT**. Latin Fragments — **ABSENT**.

### 9:7

**ወአመረ፡ ስብዓታተ፡ ስማዝያ፡ ዘአንተ፡ ወሀብኮ፡ ሥልጣነ፡ ከመ፡ ይኰንን፡ እለ፡ ምስሌሁ፡ ኅቡረ።**

*wäʾämärä səbəʿatatä səmazəya zäʾänətä wähäbəko śələṭanä kämä yəkwänənə ʾəlä məsəlehu ḫəburə*

**"And Səmazəya has (made known | disclosed | taught) the (incantations | adjurations | sworn spells | binding-oaths) — he to whom thou gavest (authority | power | dominion) that he should (rule | judge | govern | pass sentence on) those who are with him together."**

- ⭐ `ስብዓታተ` *səbəʿatatä* — the same word established at 7:1 and 8:3 in `translations/enoch-7-8.md`: a **sworn** spell, from the root "to swear," rendered `ἐπαοιδάς` in the Greek of 7:1. It occurs at exactly three places in the whole book — **7:1, 8:3, 9:7** (`geezroot.py`) — and this is the third.
- ⭐⭐ **This verse is where the Ge'ez corrects itself.** Ge'ez 8:3 attributes the spells to `አሜዛራክ`; Ge'ez 9:7 attributes them to `ስማዝያ`/Semihazah, agreeing with the Greek of 8:3 (`Σεμιαζᾶς`) against the Ge'ez of 8:3. The older attribution survives one chapter downstream of where it was lost.
- ⚠️ `ይኰንን` — the verb spans **to rule / to govern** and **to judge / to condemn**. Gizeh's `ἄρχειν` selects only "to rule." Both printed.

**Witnesses:**
- ⭐ **The manuscripts do not have this clause here.** `p` and `Bertalotto` begin the verse at the name — `ሰምያዛ፡ ዘአንተ፡ ወሀብኮ…` — having placed `ወአምእረ፡ ሰብአታተ` at the **end of 9:6** (see above). The base text's verse division is not the manuscripts'.
- ⭐ **Gizeh has no equivalent of the clause at all**, in either position: `καὶ Σεμιαζᾶς, ᾧ τὴν ἐξουσίαν ἔδωκας ἄρχειν τῶν σὺν αὐτῷ ἅμα ὄντων.` — no incantations, no verb of teaching. Its nearest material is the `καὶ ἔγνωσαν ἄνθρωποι` of 9:6.
- ⭐ **Name split, three ways:** base `ስማዝያ` *Səmazəya* · `p` `Bertalotto` `ሰምያዛ` *Semyaza* · Gizeh `Σεμιαζᾶς`. The two Ge'ez forms differ by **metathesis** of the second and third syllables.
- ⚠️ `p` `Bertalotto` omit `ከመ` before `ይኰንን`.
- Qumran Aramaic — **ABSENT**. Latin Fragments — **ABSENT**.

### 9:8

**ወሖሩ፡ ኀበ፡ አዋልደ፡ ሰብእ፡ ኅቡረ፡ ወሰከቡ፡ ምስሌሆን፡ ለእልኩ፡ አንስት፡ ወረኵሱ፡ ወአግሀዱ፡ ሎን፡ እሎንተ፡ ኃጣውአ።**

*wäḥoru ḫäbä ʾäwalədä säbəʾə ḫəburə wäsäkäbu məsəlehonə läʾələku ʾänəsətə wäräkwəsu wäʾägəhädu lonə ʾəlonətä ḫaṭawəʾä*

**"And they went to the daughters of (humankind | men) together, and lay with them — with those women — and (defiled themselves | were made unclean | became polluted), and (made manifest | uncovered | disclosed) to them these sins."**

- `ረኵሱ` *räkwəsu* — the stem is intransitive/reflexive: *they became unclean*, not *they defiled the women*. The agency stays on the Watchers.
- `ኅቡረ` *ḫəburə* — "together, jointly, in common."

**Witnesses:**
- ⭐ **Where the Ge'ez has `ኅቡረ` ("together"), Gizeh has `τῆς γῆς` ("of the earth")** — `τὰς θυγατέρας τῶν ἀνθρώπων **τῆς γῆς**`. One witness qualifies the manner of going, the other the origin of the daughters. Not reconcilable; both printed.
- ⭐ **`እሎንተ` vs `πάσας`.** Ge'ez: they revealed "**these** sins" (demonstrative). Gizeh: `πάσας τὰς ἁμαρτίας`, "**all** the sins." A one-word difference that changes the scope of the disclosure from a specific set to a total one.
- ⚠️ `p` `Bertalotto` read `**ምስለ**፡ እልኩ፡ አንስት` ("**with** those women") where the base has `**ለ**እልኩ` (dative) — the base's `ለ` produces an awkward double object after `ምስሌሆን`, and the mss regularise it.
- `p` `Bertalotto` read `ኅብረ` for base `ኅቡረ`; `p` ወሐሩ / `Bertalotto` ወሖሩ; `p` ወአግሃዱ / `Bertalotto` ወአግሀዱ; both mss ኀጣውአ — orthographic.
- Qumran Aramaic — **ABSENT**. Latin Fragments — **ABSENT**.

### 9:9

**ወአንስትኒ፡ ወለዳ፡ ረዓይተ፡ ወበዝ፡ መልአት፡ ኵላ፡ ምድር፡ ደመ፡ ወዐመፃ።**

*wäʾänəsətəni wäläda räʿayətä wäbäzə mäləʾätə kwəla mədərə dämä wäʿämäṣ́a*

**"And the women bore giants, and it (multiplied | became many); the whole earth was filled with blood and (injustice | iniquity | wickedness | lawlessness)."**

- ⭐ `ረዓይተ` *räʿayətä* — Dillmann glosses the word simply **gigantes**, and cites 1 Enoch at this verse together with 15:8 and 16:1; the derived adjective `ረዐይታዊ` is *giganteus*. It is the Ge'ez word for *giants*, with no separate sense to record.
- ⚠️ `ወበዝ` — "and it/he multiplied, became numerous." The subject is unexpressed and grammatically ambiguous: it can be the giants, or the blood-and-iniquity, or impersonal. **Not resolved.**

**Witnesses:**
- ⭐⭐ **Gizeh translates the giants as `τιτᾶνας` — Titans.** This is *interpretatio graeca*: the Greek does not use `γίγαντας`, the word the Greek Old Testament uses for the *nephilim*, but imports the name of a distinct Greek mythological race. Dillmann records the corresponding Ge'ez loanword `ጢጣናኖስ` = *Τιτᾶνες, titanes, gigantes* elsewhere, but **it is not used here** — the Ge'ez has its own native word. 🛑 The Ge'ez is not a translation of what stands in Gizeh at this point; one of the two has substituted a culturally local category, and without the Aramaic there is no way to say which direction the substitution ran.
- ⭐ **Gizeh supplies an agent the Ge'ez does not have**: `**ὑφ᾽ ὧν** ὅλη ἡ γῆ ἐπλήσθη` — "**by whom** the whole earth was filled." The Ge'ez `ወበዝ፡ መልአት፡ ኵላ፡ ምድር` leaves the filling unattributed.
- ⚠️ `p` `Bertalotto` open with `**እ**ንስትኒ` (no `ወ`) and read `ረአይተ` for base `ረዓይተ`.
- `p` ወዓመፃ / `Bertalotto` ወዐመፃ — orthographic.
- Qumran Aramaic — **ABSENT**. Latin Fragments — **ABSENT**.

### 9:10

**ወይእዜኒ፡ ናሁ፡ ይጸርሑ፡ ነፍሳተ፡ እለ፡ ሞቱ፡ ወይሰክዩ፡ እስከ፡ አንቀጸ፡ ሰማይ፡ ወዐርገ፡ ገዐሮሙ፡ ወኢይክሉ፡ ወፂአ፡ እምቅድመ፡ ገጸ፡ ዐመፃ፡ ዘይትገበር፡ በዲበ፡ ምድር።**

*wäyəʾəzeni nahu yəṣärəḥu näfəsatä ʾəlä motu wäyəsäkəyu ʾəsəkä ʾänəqäṣä sämayə wäʿärəgä gäʿäromu wäʾiyəkəlu wäṣ́iʾä ʾəməqədəmä gäṣä ʿämäṣ́a zäyətəgäbärə bädibä mədərə*

**"And now, behold, the souls of those who have died cry out and (take refuge | make petition | make complaint | lay their charge | bring their suit) as far as the gate of heaven; and their (outcry | groaning | wailing | lamentation | plea for help) has gone up, and they cannot come out from before the face of the (injustice | iniquity | wickedness | lawlessness) that is being done upon the earth."**

- ⭐ `ገዐሮሙ` *gäʿäromu* — Dillmann, `ገዓር`: *clamor dolentium, querentium, opem implorantium, ploratus, gemitus, ejulatus* — **the cry of those in pain, of those lodging a complaint, of those imploring aid**; weeping, groaning, wailing. ⭐ *querentium* is the participle of the same *queri* that defines `ሰከየ` (Finding 3): the noun and the verb of this verse **are both legal-complaint vocabulary in Dillmann's Latin**, and the two reinforce each other. All senses printed.
- `ወይሰክዩ` — see Finding 3; identical range to 9:3.

**Witnesses:**
- ⭐ **The subject of "cannot come out" is different in the two witnesses.** Ge'ez `ወኢይክሉ፡ ወፂአ` is **3rd person plural** — *they* (the souls) cannot come out. Gizeh `καὶ οὐ **δύναται** ἐξελθεῖν` is **singular**, and its only available subject is `ὁ στεναγμὸς αὐτῶν`, *their groaning*. One text has trapped souls; the other has a trapped sound. Both printed; neither preferred.
- ⭐ Ge'ez `እምቅድመ፡ **ገጸ**፡ ዐመፃ` — "from before the **face** of the iniquity," **singular** and personified. Gizeh `ἀπὸ προσώπου τῶν ἐπὶ τῆς γῆς γινομένων **ἀνομημάτων**` — "from before the lawless **deeds** done on the earth," **plural** and concrete.
- ⚠️ Gizeh reads plural `τῶν πυλῶν τοῦ οὐρανοῦ` (gates) against Ge'ez singular `አንቀጸ፡ ሰማይ` — the same split as at 9:2, so it is systematic in this chapter, not accidental.
- ⚠️ `p` `Bertalotto` read `ነፍሳት` (nominative) for base `ነፍሳተ` (construct/accusative), and `ምቱ` for base `ሞቱ`.
- `p` ይጸርሑ / `Bertalotto` ይጸርኁ; `p` ወዓርገ / `Bertalotto` ወዐርገ; both mss ገዓሮሙ, ወጺአ; `p` ዓመፃ / `Bertalotto` ዐመፃ — orthographic.
- Qumran Aramaic — **ABSENT**. Latin Fragments — **ABSENT**.

### 9:11

**ወአንተ፡ ተአምር፡ ኵሎ፡ ዘእንበለ፡ ይኩን፡ ወአንተ፡ ተአምር፡ ዘንተ፡ ወዘዚኣሆሙ፡ ወአልቦ፡ ዘትነግረነ፡ ወምንት፡ መፍትው፡ ንረስዮሙ፡ በእንተ፡ ዝንቱ።**

*wäʾänətä täʾämərə kwəlo zäʾənəbälä yəkunə wäʾänətä täʾämərə zänətä wäzäziʾahomu wäʾäləbo zätənägəränä wämənətə mäfətəwə nəräsəyomu bäʾənətä zənətu*

**"And thou knowest all things before they come to be; and thou knowest this, and that which is theirs; and there is nothing thou tellest us. And what is (fitting | proper | needful | necessary | due) that we should (do to them | make of them | deal with them) concerning this?"**

- ⭐ `መፍትው` *mäfətəwə* — Dillmann's range is wide and Dillmann **cites this verse** under the last sense: 1) *desiderabilis, exoptatus* — desirable, longed for; a) *jucundus, amoenus, praestans, eximius, pretiosus, carus* — pleasant, excellent, precious; b) *necessarius*; c) *conveniens, decorus* — fitting, seemly; **d) *reus* — guilty, liable**; 2) *decet, convenit, oportet* — it is fitting, it is proper, one must — **and Hen. 9,11 and 15,2 are Dillmann's citations for sense 2.** ⚠️ Sense (d) *reus* is recorded because Dillmann records it, and its presence in the same entry as "what one ought to do" is not nothing in a chapter that is a legal complaint — but nothing is built on it here.
- `ንረስዮሙ` — 1st plural subjunctive with 3rd masc. plural object: "that **we** should do to **them**." See below; this is the crux of the verse.

**Witnesses:**
- ⭐⭐⭐ **The question the angels ask is not the same question in the two witnesses.**

  | | who acts | on whom |
  |---|---|---|
  | **Ge'ez** `ንረስዮሙ` | **we** (1st pl.) | **them** (obj. suffix) |
  | **Gizeh** `τί δεῖ ποιεῖν αὐτοὺς` | **they** (`αὐτούς` as acc. subject of the infinitive) | — |

  Ge'ez: *"what ought **we to do to them**?"* — the archangels ask for their own orders.
  Gizeh: *"what ought **they to do**?"* — the archangels ask what the Watchers are supposed to do.
  🛑 These are different questions with different answers, and chapter 10 answers the **Ge'ez** one: it is the archangels who are given instructions. Recorded as a divergence; **not harmonised.**
- ⭐ **Gizeh has a clause the Ge'ez lacks**: `καὶ **ἐᾷς αὐτούς**` — "and thou **sufferest / permittest** them." No Ge'ez witness has it. It converts the complaint into an accusation of divine inaction.
- ⭐ **Ge'ez has a clause the Greek lacks**: `ወዘዚኣሆሙ` — "and that which is **theirs**." Gizeh's `ταῦτα` covers only "these things."
- ⚠️ **Verb split:** Ge'ez `ተአምር` (**thou knowest**) twice; Gizeh `οἶδας` (knowest) then `ὁρᾷς` (**thou seest**). The second occurrence differs.
- `p` `Bertalotto` read `ወዘዚአሆሙ` for base `ወዘዚኣሆሙ` — orthographic; otherwise the manuscripts agree with the base text throughout this verse.
- Qumran Aramaic — **ABSENT**. Latin Fragments — **ABSENT**.

# CHAPTER 10 — The Sentence

## ⭐⭐⭐ FINDING 5: `ኤልያስ` at 10:19 is a Greek word the Ge'ez read as a man's name

Ge'ez 10:19 reads `ወአሐቲ፡ መስፈርተ፡ **ኤልያስ**፡ ትገብር፡ ዐሠርተ፡ ምክያዳተ፡ ዘይት` — literally *"and one measure of **ʾElyas** shall make ten pressings of oil."* `ኤልያስ` is the standard Ge'ez spelling of the prophet's name. In a list of agricultural yields it is meaningless.

Gizeh at the same point reads **`ἐλαίας`** — *"of olive."*

⭐⭐⭐ **`ኤልያስ` is `ἐλαίας` taken over untranslated and vocalised as a proper name.** The Greek genitive of "olive" and the Ge'ez form of "Elias" are the same string of consonants. The Ge'ez translator did not recognise the common noun, transliterated it, and produced *a measure of Elias*.

🛑 This is direct internal evidence of **the direction of transmission** — a Greek word surviving as a corruption inside the Ge'ez, which cannot happen the other way round. It is one of the few places in these chapters where the provenance chain can be *observed* rather than assumed. `p` and `Bertalotto` read `ኤልያሰ`, an accusative ending on the same misread noun — the manuscripts inflect the error rather than correct it.

⚠️ The same verse contains a **second** artifact of the same kind: Ge'ez `ምክያዳተ` (*pressings, treadings*, from the root "to tread") against Gizeh `βάτους` (*baths*, a fixed liquid measure). A unit of volume has become an act of pressing.

## ⭐⭐⭐ FINDING 6: Ge'ez 10:4 names Raphael — so the Ge'ez knows him, and still left him out of 9:1

At 9:1 the Ge'ez four are Michael, Gabriel, Suruyal, Uriel, and Gizeh has Ῥαφαήλ where the Ge'ez has ሱሩያል (Finding 1). It would be easy to conclude that the Ge'ez tradition simply lacks Raphael.

**It does not.** Ge'ez 10:4 opens `ወይቤሎ፡ ካዕበ፡ እግዚእ፡ **ለሩፋኤል**` — "and again the Lord said **to Rufaʾel**" — against Gizeh `καὶ τῷ **Ῥαφαὴλ** εἶπεν`. The two agree exactly. All three Ge'ez witnesses have the name.

⭐ So the Ge'ez has a **four-name list in 9:1 that omits an archangel it names three verses later**, and puts `ሱሩያል` in the slot Gizeh gives to Raphael. And the commissions that follow in chapter 10 are given to **Raphael** (10:4), **Gabriel** (10:9) and **Michael** (10:11) — three of the four, and `ሱሩያል`/`ኡርያል` is never addressed again in the chapter. The Ge'ez list of 9:1 is internally inconsistent with the Ge'ez narrative of chapter 10. 🛑 **Recorded as a fact about the Ge'ez, not repaired.** With no Aramaic there is nothing to repair it against.

## ⭐⭐ FINDING 7: `ትጉሃን` and `ἐγρήγοροι` are the same word, and it is the one technical term that survives the whole chain intact

The Watchers are `ትጉሃן` *təguhan* in Ge'ez (10:7, 10:9, 10:15) and `ἐγρήγοροι` in Gizeh at every one of those places.

- `ትጉህ` is the participle of `ተግሀ`, "to be awake, to keep watch." Dillmann's citations for the verb render Greek **γρηγοροῦντες** — *"they were keeping watch"* (of the shepherds, and of the householder who watches for the thief).
- `ἐγρήγορος` is the participle of the same Greek verb.

⭐ **Both languages use their own native word for "the wakeful ones," and they match.** In a chapter where the archangel list, the topography, the numbers, the measures and the very question being asked all diverge, the title of the offending class is transmitted without loss. It is a translated term in Ge'ez, not a borrowing — the Ge'ez did **not** transliterate ἐγρήγοροι, it re-made it.

⚠️ Contrast `መንዝራን` at 10:9 (below), where **neither** language translated.

## ⭐⭐ FINDING 8: Seventy generations or human generations — a one-letter split inside the Ge'ez

Ge'ez 10:12, the length of the Watchers' binding:

| Witness | Reading | Means |
|---|---|---|
| Ge'ez base | `ለ**ሰብዓ**፡ ትውልድ` | for **seventy** generations |
| Ge'ez `p` `Bertalotto` | `ለ**ሰብእ**፡ ትውልድ` | for the generation(s) **of man** |
| Greek Gizeh | `**ἑβδομήκοντα** γενεὰς` | for **seventy** generations |

⭐ `ሰብዓ` (seventy) and `ሰብእ` (man, humankind) differ by **one letter**, and the difference converts a fixed term of sentence into an indefinite one. Gizeh sides with the base text. 🛑 That does not settle it — the manuscripts are the physical witnesses and the base text is an edition — so **both are printed and neither is preferred.**

## ⭐⭐ FINDING 9: The two texts do not put the Watchers in the same landscape

Three separate prepositional and topographic reversals, one per commission:

| | Ge'ez | Greek Gizeh | |
|---|---|---|---|
| 10:5 | `ወደይ፡ **ላዕሌሁ**፡ አእባነ` — place stones **upon** him | `**ὑπόθες** αὐτῷ λίθους` — place stones **under** him | ⭐ opposite |
| 10:12 | `በመትሕተ፡ **አውግረ**፡ ምድር` — beneath the **hills** of the earth | `εἰς τὰς **νάπας** τῆς γῆς` — into the **glens/valleys** of the earth | ⭐ opposite |
| 10:3 | `ወይንበር፡ ዘርዑ፡ **በኵሉ፡ ምድር**` — his seed shall remain **in all the earth** | `μενεῖ τὸ σπέρμα αὐτοῦ **εἰς πάσας τὰς γενεὰς τοῦ αἰῶνος**` — **unto all the generations of the age** | ⭐ **space vs time** |

`አውግር` is the plural Dillmann pairs with `ደብር` (mountain) and which renders Greek **βουνοί**, *hills* — it is not a word for a valley. `νάπη` is not a word for a hill. The Ge'ez buries Azazel's fellows **under high ground**; the Greek puts them **into low ground**.

⭐⭐ The 10:3 split is the sharpest: the survival promised to Noah's seed is **geographical** in one text and **chronological** in the other. `p` and `Bertalotto` read `ለኵሉ፡ ምድር` (dative) against the base `በኵሉ፡ ምድር` (locative) — the Ge'ez tradition is itself unsteady here, but never temporal.

## THE TEXT

### 10:1

**ወአሜሃ፡ ልዑል፡ ዐቢይ፡ ወቅዱስ፡ ተናገረ፡ ወፈነዎ፡ ለአርስየላልዩር፡ ኀበ፡ ወልደ፡ ላሜክ።**

*wäʾämeha ləʿulə ʿäbiyə wäqədusə tänagärä wäfänäwo läʾärəsəyälaləyurə ḫäbä wälədä lamekə*

**And then the Most High, the Great and Holy One, spoke, and sent ʾArsəyälaləyur to the son of Lamek.**

**Witnesses:**
- ⭐⭐ **The messenger has two entirely different names.** Ge'ez `አርስየላልዩር` *ʾArsəyälaləyur* (all three Ge'ez witnesses) against Gizeh `Ἰστραήλ` *Istraēl*. These are not variants of one another — they share no syllable structure. 🛑 **Both printed. Neither preferred.**
- ⭐ **Gizeh has a longer verb chain and a phrase the Ge'ez lacks**: `Τότε Ὕψιστος **εἶπεν περὶ τούτων**, ὁ μέγας Ἅγιος, καὶ ἐλάλησεν **καὶ εἶπεν** καὶ ἔπεμψεν` — "Then the Most High **spoke concerning these things**, the Great Holy One, and he uttered **and said** and sent." Three verbs of speech plus `περὶ τούτων`; the Ge'ez has one (`ተናገረ`).
- ⚠️ Gizeh's word order puts `ὁ μέγας Ἅγιος` **after** the verb, as an apposition; the Ge'ez has `ልዑል፡ ዐቢይ፡ ወቅዱስ` stacked before it.
- ⭐ `p` `Bertalotto` continue the verse with `**ወይቤሎ፤**` ("and he said to him") which the base text assigns to the opening of 10:2 — a verse-division difference inside the Ge'ez tradition.
- `p` ዓቢይ / `Bertalotto` ዐቢይ — orthographic.
- Qumran Aramaic — **ABSENT**. Latin Fragments — **ABSENT**.

### 10:2

**ወይቤሎ፡ በሎ፡ በስመ፡ ዚኣየ፡ ኅባእ፡ ርእሰከ፡ ወአግህድ፡ ሎቱ፡ ፍጻሜ፡ ዘይመጽእ፡ እስመ፡ ኵላ፡ ምድር፡ ትትሀጐል፡ ወማየ፡ አይኅ፡ ይመጽእ፡ ሀሎ፡ ዲበ፡ ኵላ፡ ምድር፡ ወይትሀጐል፡ ዘሀሎ፡ ውስቴታ።**

*wäyəbelo bälo bäsəmä ziʾayä ḫəbaʾə rəʾəsäkä wäʾägəhədə lotu fəṣame zäyəmäṣəʾə ʾəsəmä kwəla mədərə tətəhägwälə wämayä ʾäyəḫə yəmäṣəʾə hälo dibä kwəla mədərə wäyətəhägwälə zähälo wəsəteta*

**And he said to him: "Say to him in my own name: 'Hide thyself' — and (make manifest | disclose | reveal) to him the (end | consummation | completion) that is coming; for the whole earth shall (perish | be destroyed | be ruined), and the water of the flood is about to come upon the whole earth, and that which is in her shall (perish | be destroyed)."**

- `ፍጻሜ` *fəṣame* — the noun of completion: an end, a fulfilment, a consummation. Gizeh `τέλος` has the identical double range.
- `አግህድ` — the imperative of the verb whose participle `ግሁድ` Dillmann glosses *manifestus, apertus, evidens, omnibus notus et perspectus* (see 9:5).

**Witnesses:**
- ⭐ **Voice differs at the close.** Ge'ez `ወይትሀጐል፡ ዘሀሎ፡ ውስቴታ` is **intransitive/passive** — "that which is in her shall perish." Gizeh `καὶ **ἀπολέσει** πάντα ὅσα ἐστιν ἐν αὐτῇ` is **active** — "and [the flood] **shall destroy** everything that is in her." One text has the world dying; the other has the water killing it.
- ⚠️ Gizeh adds `πάντα` ("**everything** that is in her"); the Ge'ez relative `ዘሀሎ፡ ውስቴታ` is unquantified.
- ⚠️ `p` `Bertalotto` invert to `ምድር፡ ኵላ` where the base has `ኵላ፡ ምድር`.
- `p` `Bertalotto` ዚአየ; `p` ትትኃጐል, ወይትኃጐል / `Bertalotto` ትትሀጐል, ወይትሀጐል — orthographic.
- Qumran Aramaic — **ABSENT**. Latin Fragments — **ABSENT**.

### 10:3

**ወይእዜኒ፡ መሀሮ፡ ከመ፡ ይንፍጽ፡ ወይንበር፡ ዘርዑ፡ በኵሉ፡ ምድር።**

*wäyəʾəzeni mähäro kämä yənəfəṣə wäyənəbärə zärəʿu bäkwəlu mədərə*

**"And now, teach him that he may (escape | slip away | get clear), and his seed shall (remain | abide | be established | dwell) in all the earth."**

**Witnesses:**
- ⭐⭐ **See Finding 9.** Ge'ez `በኵሉ፡ ምድር` — *in all the earth*, **spatial**. Gizeh `εἰς πάσας τὰς γενεὰς τοῦ αἰῶνος` — *unto all the generations of the age*, **temporal**. There is no reading that is both.
- ⚠️ `p` `Bertalotto` read `**ለ**ኵሉ፡ ምድር` (dative, "to/for all the earth") against base `**በ**ኵሉ፡ ምድር` (locative). The Ge'ez tradition is unsteady on the preposition but never on the noun.
- Gizeh `ἐκφύγη` — *escape, flee out*. ⭐ Note for 10:17 below: **Gizeh uses this same verb there, and the Ge'ez does not.**
- `p` `Bertalotto` መሐሮ, ዘርኡ — orthographic.
- Qumran Aramaic — **ABSENT**. Latin Fragments — **ABSENT**.

### 10:4

**ወይቤሎ፡ ካዕበ፡ እግዚእ፡ ለሩፋኤል፡ እስሮ፡ ለአዛዝኤል፡ በእደዊሁ፡ ወበእግሪሁ፡ ወደዮ፡ ውስተ፡ ጽልመት፡ ወአብቅዋ፡ ለገዳም፡ እንተ፡ ሀለወት፡ በዱዳኤል፡ ወደዮ፡ ውስተ፡ ጽልመት፡ በህየ።**

*wäyəbelo kaʿəbä ʾəgəziʾə lärufaʾelə ʾəsəro läʾäzazəʾelə bäʾədäwihu wäbäʾəgərihu wädäyo wəsətä ṣələmätə wäʾäbəqəwa lägädamə ʾənətä häläwätə bädudaʾelə wädäyo wəsətä ṣələmätə bähəyä*

**And again the Lord said to Rufaʾel: "Bind ʾAzazəʾel by his hands and by his feet, and cast him into the darkness; and (split open | cleave | make an opening in) the (wilderness | desert | solitude) which is in Dudaʾel, and cast him into the darkness there."**

- ⭐ `ለሩፋኤል` — **see Finding 6.** The Ge'ez names Raphael here and not at 9:1.
- `ገዳም` *gädam* — the uninhabited place: desert, wilderness, solitude. Gizeh `ἔρημον`, the same range.

**Witnesses:**
- ⭐ **Limb order is reversed.** Ge'ez `በእደዊሁ፡ ወበእግሪሁ` — **hands** then **feet**. Gizeh `ποσὶν καὶ χερσίν` — **feet** then **hands**.
- ⚠️ **The base text repeats `ውስተ፡ ጽልመት` and no other witness does.** Base: `ወደዮ፡ **ውስተ፡ ጽልመት**፡ በህየ` at the close. `p` `Bertalotto`: `ወደዮ፡ **ህየ**` — just "cast him there." Gizeh: `κἀκεῖ βάλε αὐτόν` — "and there cast him." **The manuscripts and the Greek agree against the base text**, which is the pattern of a dittography from the first `ወደዮ፡ ውስተ፡ ጽልመት` earlier in the same verse. Recorded, not emended.
- ⭐ **Name split:** Ge'ez `ዱዳኤል` *Dudaʾel* against Gizeh `Δαδουήλ` *Dadouēl* — the vowels differ in both syllables.
- ⚠️ Ge'ez `አዛዝኤል` against Gizeh `Ἀζαήλ` — the same split as at 9:6, so it is systematic.
- ⚠️ `p` `Bertalotto` read `ወ**እገ**ሪሁ` (without the second `በ`) for base `ወ**በእግ**ሪሁ`.
- Qumran Aramaic — **ABSENT**. Latin Fragments — **ABSENT**.

### 10:5

**ወደይ፡ ላዕሌሁ፡ አእባነ፡ ጠዋያተ፡ ወበሊኃተ፡ ወክድኖ፡ ጽልመተ፡ ወህየ፡ ይኅድር፡ ለዓለም፡ ወክድኖ፡ ለገጹ፡ ከመ፡ ኢይርአይ፡ ብርሃነ።**

*wädäyə laʿəlehu ʾäʾəbanä ṭäwayatä wäbäliḫatä wäkədəno ṣələmätä wähəyä yəḫədərə läʿalämə wäkədəno lägäṣu kämä ʾiyərəʾäyə bərəhanä*

**"And lay upon him stones (rough | twisted | crooked) and sharp, and cover him with darkness; and there let him dwell for ever; and cover his face, that he may not see light."**

**Witnesses:**
- ⭐⭐ **The preposition is reversed — see Finding 9.** Ge'ez `ወደይ፡ **ላዕሌሁ**` — *lay **upon** him*. Gizeh `**ὑπόθες** αὐτῷ` — *lay **under** him*. `ὑποτίθημι` is unambiguously "place beneath." 🛑 In one text the stones are a covering; in the other they are a bed. **Both printed.**
- ⚠️ Ge'ez `ወክድኖ፡ **ለገጹ**` — cover **his face**. Gizeh `τὴν **ὄψιν** αὐτοῦ **πώμασον**` — *"**stopper / plug** his sight."* `πωμάζω` is to seal with a lid; the Ge'ez verb is simply "to cover," the same verb used two clauses earlier for the darkness. Greek escalates, Ge'ez repeats.
- `ጠዋያተ` — the root spans **twisted, crooked, bent** as well as **rough**; Gizeh's `τραχεῖς` selects "rough/jagged." Both printed.
- `p` አዕባነ, ወበሊሀተ / `Bertalotto` አእባነ, ወበሊኃተ — orthographic.
- Qumran Aramaic — **ABSENT**. Latin Fragments — **ABSENT**.

### 10:6

**ወበዐባይ፡ ዕለት፡ እንተ፡ ኵነኔ፡ ከመ፡ ይትፌነው፡ ውስተ፡ ዋዕይ።**

*wäbäʿäbayə ʿəlätə ʾənətä kwənäne kämä yətəfenäwə wəsətä waʿəyə*

**"And on the great day of (judgement | condemnation | sentence) that he may be **sent** into the (burning | heat | fire)."**

**Witnesses:**
- ⭐ **Different verbs.** Ge'ez `ይትፌነው` — *he shall be **sent***, the passive of the verb used of the messenger in 10:1. Gizeh `ἀπαχθήσεται` — *he shall be **led away***, a verb of custodial removal. The Ge'ez keeps a single root for divine dispatch throughout the chapter; the Greek does not.
- ⚠️ Ge'ez word order `ወበ**ዐባይ**፡ ዕለት` (base) against `p` `Bertalotto` `ወበ**ዕለት**፡ ዓባይ` — adjective before noun in the base, after it in the manuscripts.
- ⚠️ Gizeh's `ἐν τῇ ἡμέρᾳ **τῆς μεγάλης τῆς κρίσεως**` is grammatically strained — the article-adjective agreement points to *"of the great judgement"* rather than *"the great day."* The Ge'ez unambiguously attaches "great" to **the day**. Recorded as a genuine ambiguity in the Greek, not resolved.
- `p` `Bertalotto` ይትፈነው — orthographic.
- Qumran Aramaic — **ABSENT**. Latin Fragments — **ABSENT**.

### 10:7

**ወአሕይዋ፡ ለምድር፡ እንተ፡ አማሰኑ፡ መላእክት፡ ወሕይወታ፡ ለምድር፡ አይድዕ፡ ከመ፡ አሕይዎ፡ አሕይዋ፡ ለምድር፡ ወኢይትሀጐሉ፡ ኵሎሙ፡ ውሉደ፡ ሰብእ፡ በምሥጢረ፡ ኵሉ፡ ዘቀተሉ፡ ትጉሃን፡ ወመሀሩ፡ ለውሉዶሙ።**

*wäʾäḥəyəwa lämədərə ʾənətä ʾämasänu mälaʾəkətə wäḥəyəwäta lämədərə ʾäyədəʿə kämä ʾäḥəyəwo ʾäḥəyəwa lämədərə wäʾiyətəhägwälu kwəlomu wəludä säbəʾə bäməśəṭirä kwəlu zäqätälu təguhanə wämähäru läwəludomu*

**"And (heal | make alive | restore to life) the earth which the angels have (corrupted | ruined | laid waste); and make known the (healing | life | restoration) of the earth, that they may heal her — heal the earth — and that all the children of (humankind | men) may not perish through the (mystery | secret) of all that the Watchers (killed | slew) and taught to their sons."**

- ⭐ `አሕይዋ` — the causative of the verb "to live": it means both **to heal** and **to make alive / keep alive**, and Ge'ez does not separate them. Gizeh's `ἰαθήσεται` / `ἴασιν` / `ἰάσωνται` selects "heal" three times. Both printed.
- ⭐ `ትጉሃን` — the Watchers = *the wakeful ones*. **See Finding 7.**

**Witnesses:**
- ⭐⭐ **The Watchers do two different things.** Ge'ez `ዘ**ቀተሉ**፡ ትጉሃን` — the verb is "to **kill, slay**." Gizeh `ᾧ **ἐπέταξαν** οἱ ἐγρήγοροι` — "which the Watchers **enjoined / commanded**." 🛑 A verb of homicide against a verb of instruction, in a clause whose other half is "and taught to their sons." **Neither is preferred and neither is emended toward the other.**
- ⭐ **Gizeh has a purpose clause the Ge'ez lacks**: `ἵνα **ἰάσωνται τὴν πληγήν**` — *"that they may heal **the wound / the plague**."* No Ge'ez witness has an object. ⚠️ The base text instead has `ከመ፡ **አሕይዎ፡ አሕይዋ**፡ ለምድር` — the verb written **twice**, and `p` `Bertalotto` have it once (`ከመ፡ አሕይዋ፡ ለምድር`). **The manuscripts agree with the Greek against the base's doubling.** Whether the base preserves a lost object corrupted into a repeated verb, or is simple dittography, **is not decided here.**
- ⭐ **Gizeh has a second verb of ruin the Ge'ez lacks** for the state of the earth, and the Ge'ez has a fuller subject: Ge'ez names `መላእክት` (the angels) as those who ruined it; Gizeh `οἱ ἄγγελοι` ✔ agrees.
- ⚠️ `p` `Bertalotto` read `**ለ**ምሥጢረ` (dative) against base `**በ**ምሥጢረ` (instrumental/locative); Gizeh `**ἐν** τῷ μυστηρίῳ` sides with the base.
- `p` ወኢይትኃጐሉ, ወመሐሩ / `Bertalotto` ወኢይትሀጐሉ, ወመሀሩ — orthographic.
- Qumran Aramaic — **ABSENT**. Latin Fragments — **ABSENT**.

### 10:8

**ወማሰነት፡ ኵላ፡ ምድር፡ በትምህርተ፡ ግብሩ፡ ለአዛዝኤል፡ ወላዕሌሁ፡ ጸሐፍ፡ ኵሎ፡ ኀጢአተ።**

*wämasänätə kwəla mədərə bätəməhərətä gəbəru läʾäzazəʾelə wälaʿəlehu ṣäḥäfə kwəlo ḫäṭiʾätä*

**And the whole earth has been (corrupted | ruined | laid waste) by the teaching of the work of ʾAzazəʾel; and against him write all sin.**

**Witnesses:**
- ⭐ **Gizeh has two verbs where the Ge'ez has one**: `καὶ **ἠρημώθη** πᾶσα ἡ γῆ **ἀφανισθεῖσα**` — "the whole earth **was made desolate**, having been **destroyed**." The Ge'ez `ወማሰነት` is single. A Greek plus, or a Greek doublet rendering one Semitic verb.
- ⚠️ **The genitive chain is inverted.** Ge'ez `በትምህርተ፡ **ግብሩ**፡ ለአዛዝኤል` — "by the teaching **of the work** of Azazel." Gizeh `ἐν τοῖς **ἔργοις τῆς διδασκαλίας** Ἀζαήλ` — "by **the works of the teaching** of Azazel." Teaching-of-work against works-of-teaching; a real reversal, not a stylistic one.
- ⚠️ Gizeh `τὰς ἁμαρτίας **πάσας**` (plural sins) against Ge'ez `ኵሎ፡ ኀጢአተ` (collective singular).
- `p` `Bertalotto` ኃጢአተ — orthographic.
- Qumran Aramaic — **ABSENT**. Latin Fragments — **ABSENT**.

### 10:9

**ወለገብርኤል፡ ይቤሎ፡ እግዚአብሔር፡ ሖር፡ ዲቤሆሙ፡ ለመንዝራን፡ ወለምኑናን፡ ወዲበ፡ ውሉደ፡ ዘማ፡ ወአህጕሎሙ፡ ለውሉደ፡ ዘማ፡ ወለውሉደ፡ ትጉሃን፡ እምሰብእ፡ ወአውፅኦሙ፡ ወፈንዎሙ፡ በበይናቲሆሙ፡ ወእሙንቱ፡ በቀትል፡ ለሊሆሙ፡ ይትሀጐሉ፡ እስመ፡ ኑኀ፡ መዋዕል፡ አልቦሙ።**

*wälägäbərəʾelə yəbelo ʾəgəziʾäbəḥerə ḥorə dibehomu lämänəzəranə wälämənunanə wädibä wəludä zäma wäʾähəgwəlomu läwəludä zäma wäläwəludä təguhanə ʾəməsäbəʾə wäʾäwəṣ́əʾomu wäfänəwomu bäbäyənatihomu wäʾəmunətu bäqätələ lälihomu yətəhägwälu ʾəsəmä nuḫä mäwaʿələ ʾäləbomu*

**And to Gabrəʾel the Lord said: "Go against the *mänzəran*, and against the (rejected | reprobate | adulterine | spurious | repudiated | despised | worthless) ones, and against the children of fornication; and destroy the children of fornication and the children of the Watchers from among (humankind | men); and bring them out and send them one against another, and they themselves shall perish in battle by their own hands — for length of days is not theirs."**

- ⭐⭐ `መንዝራን` *mänzəran* — **Dillmann has no entry for this word.** Gizeh does not translate it either: `τοὺς **μαζηρέους**`, a Greek transliteration with a Greek plural ending stuck on a Semitic stem. 🛑 **Two independent translators both declined to render it**, which is what happens to a technical term of status with no equivalent in the receiving language. Its sense is **not recoverable from the tools available here** and is not guessed at. It is left transliterated, as both ancient witnesses left it.
- ⭐ `ምኑናን` *mənunan* — Dillmann's `ምኑን`, and **he cites 1 Enoch 10:9 in the entry**: 1) *rejectus, reprobatus et rejiciendus, reprobandus* — rejected, reprobate, to be rejected — and within that sense he glosses **adulterinus**, *adulterine, of illegitimate birth*, which is where the Enoch citation sits; 2) *repudiatus, spretus, abominabilis*; 3) *vilis, futilis, nequam (inanis)* — worthless, futile, good-for-nothing. Gizeh `κιβδήλους` — *adulterated, debased, counterfeit* — falls inside that range. **All senses printed.**
- `ዘማ` *zäma* — fornication, harlotry.

**Witnesses:**
- ⭐ **The Ge'ez destroys two classes; the Greek one.** Ge'ez `ወአህጕሎሙ፡ **ለውሉደ፡ ዘማ**፡ ወ**ለውሉደ፡ ትጉሃን**` — the children of fornication **and** the children of the Watchers. Gizeh `ἀπόλεσον **τοὺς υἱοὺς τῶν ἐγρηγόρων**` — the Watchers' children only. The Greek has "children of fornication" earlier, in the go-against list, and does not repeat it.
- ⭐ **Ge'ez has a command Gizeh lacks**: `ወ**አውፅኦሙ**` — "and **bring them out**." No Greek counterpart at any point in the verse.
- ⭐ **Ge'ez has a self-destruction clause Gizeh compresses to two words.** Ge'ez: `ወፈንዎሙ፡ በበይናቲሆሙ፡ ወእሙንቱ፡ በቀትል፡ **ለሊሆሙ**፡ ይትሀጐሉ` — "send them **one against another**, and they shall perish in battle **by their own selves**." Gizeh: `πέμψον αὐτοὺς **ἐν πολέμῳ ἀπωλείας**` — "send them **in a war of destruction**," with no reciprocal and no reflexive.
- ⚠️ `p` `Bertalotto` reorder the clause to `በበይናቲሆሙ፡ **እሙንቱ፡ ወለሊሆሙ፡ በቀትል**፡ ይትሀጐሉ` — the reflexive and the instrumental swap places.
- `p` `Bertalotto` read ሑር for base ሖር, and መንዚራን for base መንዝራን.
- `p` ወአኅጕሎሙ, ይትኃጐሉ, ኑኃ / `Bertalotto` ወአህጕሎሙ, ይትሀጐሉ, ኑኀ — orthographic.
- Qumran Aramaic — **ABSENT**. Latin Fragments — **ABSENT**.

### 10:10

**ወኵሎሙ፡ ይስእሉከ፡ ወኢይከውን፡ ለአበዊሆሙ፡ በእንቲኣሆሙ፡ እስመ፡ ይሴፈዉ፡ ሕይወተ፡ ዘለዓለም፡ ወከመ፡ ይሕየዉ፡ ሕይወተ፡ አሐዱ፡ አሐዱ፡ እምኔሆሙ፡ ኀምስተ፡ ምእተ፡ ክራማተ።**

*wäkwəlomu yəsəʾəlukä wäʾiyəkäwənə läʾäbäwihomu bäʾənətiʾahomu ʾəsəmä yəsefäwu ḥəyəwätä zäläʿalämə wäkämä yəḥəyäwu ḥəyəwätä ʾäḥädu ʾäḥädu ʾəmənehomu ḫäməsətä məʾətä kəramatä*

**"And they shall all (ask | petition | entreat | beg) thee, and it shall not be for their fathers on their behalf; for they hope for life everlasting, and that each one of them shall live a life of five hundred years."**

**Witnesses:**
- ⭐⭐ **The first clause is a verb in one text and a noun in the other, and only one of them has a second person.** Ge'ez `ወኵሎሙ፡ **ይስእሉከ**` — "and they all shall **petition thee**," addressed to Gabriel. Gizeh `καὶ **πᾶσα ἐρώτησις** οὐκ ἔσται τοῖς πατράσιν αὐτῶν` — "and **every request** shall not be for their fathers," with no addressee and the negation carried across the whole. 🛑 In the Ge'ez the petition is **made** and then refused for the fathers; in the Greek there is **no petition at all**. Both printed.
- ⚠️ Gizeh `καὶ **περὶ** αὐτῶν` — "and **concerning** them" — matches Ge'ez `በእንቲኣሆሙ`, but the Greek joins it to the negated clause with `καί` where the Ge'ez has it as a bare adjunct.
- ⭐ **The number agrees exactly**: Ge'ez `ኀምስተ፡ ምእተ፡ ክራማተ` = five hundred years; Gizeh `ἔτη πεντακόσια` = five hundred years. ⚠️ `p` `Bertalotto` write it with **numerals** — `፭፻` — and also `፩፩` for `አሐዱ፡ አሐዱ`.
- 🛑 **`p` and `Bertalotto` carry a displaced word**: they read `ወከመ፡ ይሕየዉ፡ ፩፩፡ **እም(ሕይወተ)፡ ኔሆሙ**፡ ፭፻፡ ክራማተ` — the word `ሕይወተ` ("a life") has been **inserted into the middle of `እምኔሆሙ`**, splitting it. The base text has `ይሕየዉ፡ **ሕይወተ**፡ አሐዱ፡ አሐዱ፡ **እምኔሆሙ**` in the ordinary order. A mechanical scribal intrusion, recorded because it shows the manuscripts' state, not corrected.
- `p` `Bertalotto` በእንቲአሆሙ — orthographic.
- Qumran Aramaic — **ABSENT**. Latin Fragments — **ABSENT**.

### 10:11

**ወለሚካኤል፡ ይቤሎ፡ እግዚአብሔር፡ ሑር፡ አይድዕ፡ ለስምያዛ፡ ወለካልኣን፡ እለ፡ ምስሌሁ፡ እለ፡ ኀብሩ፡ ምስለ፡ አንስት፡ ከመ፡ ይማስኑ፡ ምስሌሆን፡ በኵሉ፡ ርኵሰ፡ ዚኣሆን።**

*wälämikaʾelə yəbelo ʾəgəziʾäbəḥerə ḥurə ʾäyədəʿə läsəməyaza wäläkaləʾanə ʾəlä məsəlehu ʾəlä ḫäbəru məsəlä ʾänəsətə kämä yəmasənu məsəlehonə bäkwəlu rəkwəsä ziʾahonə*

**And to Mikaʾel the Lord said: "Go, make known to Səməyaza and to the others who are with him, who (joined | associated | united) themselves with the women, so as to be (corrupted | defiled | ruined) with them in all their uncleanness."**

**Witnesses:**
- ⭐ **Gizeh drops the subject.** Ge'ez `ወለሚካኤል፡ ይቤሎ፡ **እግዚአብሔር**` — "and to Michael **the Lord** said," exactly parallel to 10:9. Gizeh `καὶ εἶπεν Μιχαήλ` — no `ὁ κύριος`, though it had it three verses earlier at `καὶ τῷ Γαβριὴλ εἶπεν **ὁ κύριος**`. ⚠️ Without it, `Μιχαήλ` is unmarked for case and the Greek can be read as "**and Michael said**" — the addressee becoming the speaker. **The Ge'ez has no such ambiguity.** Recorded; not resolved.
- ⭐ **The dative marker is missing in the Greek too**: Gizeh `εἶπεν Μιχαήλ` against Ge'ez `**ለ**ሚካኤል`. The one Greek witness gives no help here.
- `p` `Bertalotto` read `ወለሚካኤል**ኒ**` (with the additive enclitic — "and **also** to Michael"), and `ምስ**ሎ**ሁ` for base `ምስሌሁ`.
- `p` ኃብሩ / `Bertalotto` ኀብሩ; `p` `Bertalotto` ዚእሆን — orthographic.
- Qumran Aramaic — **ABSENT**. Latin Fragments — **ABSENT**.

### 10:12

**ሶበ፡ ይትራገዙ፡ ኵሉ፡ ውሉዶሙ፡ ወሶበ፡ ይሬእዩ፡ ሀጕሎሙ፡ ለፍቁራኒሆሙ፡ እስሮሙ፡ ለሰብዓ፡ ትውልድ፡ በመትሕተ፡ አውግረ፡ ምድር፡ እስከ፡ ዕለተ፡ ኵነኔሆሙ፡ ወተፍጻሜቶሙ፡ እስከ፡ ይትፌጸም፡ ኵነኔ፡ ዘለዓለመ፡ ዓለም።**

*sobä yətəragäzu kwəlu wəludomu wäsobä yəreʾəyu hägwəlomu läfəquranihomu ʾəsəromu läsäbəʿa təwələdə bämätəḥətä ʾäwəgərä mədərə ʾəsəkä ʿəlätä kwənänehomu wätäfəṣametomu ʾəsəkä yətəfeṣämə kwənäne zäläʿalämä ʿalämə*

**"When all their sons (run one another through | pierce one another | are slaughtered), and when they see the destruction of their (beloved | dear ones), bind them for seventy generations beneath the hills of the earth, until the day of their (judgement | condemnation | sentence) and their consummation, until the (judgement | condemnation | sentence) of eternity of eternities is completed."**

**Witnesses:**
- ⭐⭐ **`ሰብዓ` (seventy) against `ሰብእ` (man) — see Finding 8.** `p` `Bertalotto` read `ለ**ሰብእ**፡ ትውልድ`; the base and Gizeh (`ἑβδομήκοντα`) read seventy. **Both printed; neither preferred.**
- ⭐⭐ **`አውግረ` (hills) against `νάπας` (glens) — see Finding 9.** The two texts put the binding at opposite elevations.
- ⚠️ **Voice differs on the sons' death.** Ge'ez `ይትራገዙ` is **reciprocal** — *they pierce/run through one another*, matching `በበይናቲሆሙ` at 10:9. Gizeh `κατασφαγῶσιν` is **passive** — *they are slaughtered*, agent unnamed. The Ge'ez keeps the mutual-slaughter theme of 10:9; the Greek drops it in both verses.
- ⚠️ Gizeh `μέχρι ἡμέρας κρίσεως αὐτῶν καὶ **συντελεσμοῦ**` — the consummation is left ungoverned in Greek; Ge'ez `ወተፍጻሜቶሙ` has the suffix ("**their** consummation").
- `p` ኃጕሎሙ / `Bertalotto` ሀጕሎሙ — orthographic.
- Qumran Aramaic — **ABSENT**. Latin Fragments — **ABSENT**.

### 10:13

**ወበውእቱ፡ መዋዕል፡ ይወስድዎሙ፡ ውስተ፡ መትሕተ፡ እሳት፡ በጻዕር፡ ወበቤተ፡ ሞቅሕ፡ ይትዐጸዉ፡ ለዓለመ፡ ዓለም።**

*wäbäwəʾətu mäwaʿələ yəwäsədəwomu wəsətä mätəḥətä ʾəsatə bäṣaʿərə wäbäbetä moqəḥə yətəʿäṣäwu läʿalämä ʿalämə*

**"And in those days they shall lead them away into the (depth | abyss | lowest part) of fire, in (torment | anguish | travail), and in the house of (bonds | fetters | imprisonment) they shall be shut up for ever and ever."**

**Witnesses:**
- ⚠️ **Finite verbs in Ge'ez, a genitive chain in Greek.** Ge'ez `ወበቤተ፡ ሞቅሕ፡ **ይትዐጸዉ**፡ ለዓለመ፡ ዓለም` — *"in the prison-house **they shall be shut up** for ever and ever."* Gizeh `εἰς τὸ δεσμωτήριον **συνκλείσεως αἰῶνος**` — *"into the prison **of the shutting-up of the age**,"* with the shutting turned into a noun and no verb at all. Same information, incompatible grammar.
- ⚠️ Ge'ez `መትሕተ፡ እሳት` — the *lower part / depth* of fire. Gizeh `τὸ **χάος** τοῦ πυρός` — the *chasm / void* of fire. `መትሕት` is a relational term (what is beneath); `χάος` is a thing. Both printed.
- `p` በፃዕር, ይትአፀዉ / `Bertalotto` በጻዕር, ይትዐፀዉ — orthographic; note **all three Ge'ez forms of the verb differ** (`ይትዐጸዉ` / `ይትአፀዉ` / `ይትዐፀዉ`).
- Qumran Aramaic — **ABSENT**. Latin Fragments — **ABSENT**.

### 10:14

**ወሶቤሃ፡ ይውዒ፡ ወይማስን፡ እምይእዜ፡ ወእስከ፡ ለዓለም፡ ምስሌሆሙ፡ ኅቡረ፡ ወይትአሰሩ፡ እስከ፡ ተፍጻሜተ፡ ትውልደ፡ ትውልድ።**

*wäsobeha yəwəʿi wäyəmasənə ʾəməyəʾəze wäʾəsəkä läʿalämə məsəlehomu ḫəburä wäyətəʾäsäru ʾəsəkä täfəṣametä təwələdä təwələdə*

**"And then he shall burn and be (destroyed | corrupted | ruined) from now and unto eternity; together with them they shall be bound until the consummation of generation of generations."**

**Witnesses:**
- ⭐ **Ge'ez has a temporal adverb where Gizeh has a relative pronoun.** Ge'ez `ወ**ሶቤሃ**` — "and **then**," a narrative connective. Gizeh `καὶ **ὃς ἂν** κατακαυθῇ` — "and **whoever** shall be burned up," a generalising relative. 🛑 The Ge'ez states that a particular burning happens; the Greek describes a class of persons. The subject of the Ge'ez verb `ይውዒ` (3rd masc. sing.) is **unexpressed and ambiguous** — Azazel from 10:6, or a collective. **Not resolved.**
- ⭐ **`p` `Bertalotto` lack `ወእስከ፡ ለዓለም`** ("and unto eternity"), reading `እምይእዜ፡ ምስሌሆሙ፡ ኅቡረ`. Gizeh likewise has only `ἀπὸ τοῦ νῦν` with no "unto eternity." **The manuscripts and the Greek agree against the base text.**
- ⚠️ `p` `Bertalotto` also drop the `ወ` from `ወይትአሰሩ`.
- ⚠️ Gizeh `μέχρι τελειώσεως **γενεᾶς**` — **singular** generation. Ge'ez `ተፍጻሜተ፡ **ትውልደ፡ ትውልድ**` — *generation of generations*, a Semitic superlative. Different scope.
- Qumran Aramaic — **ABSENT**. Latin Fragments — **ABSENT**.

### 10:15

**ወአህጕሎሙ፡ ለኵሎሙ፡ ነፍሳተ፡ ተውኔት፡ ወለውሉዶሙኒ፡ ለትጉሃን፡ እስመ፡ ገፍዕዎሙ፡ ለሰብእ።**

*wäʾähəgwəlomu läkwəlomu näfəsatä täwənetə wäläwəludomuni lätəguhanə ʾəsəmä gäfəʿəwomu läsäbəʾə*

**"And destroy all the souls of (lust | pleasure | wanton delight | sport | play | amusement) and the sons of the Watchers as well, because they have (oppressed | wronged | done violence to | injured) (humankind | men)."**

- ⭐⭐ `ተውኔት` *täwnet* — Dillmann, from `ተዋነየ` *ludere* (to play): 1) **oblectatio, voluptas, libido** — delight, pleasure, **lust** — ⭐ **and Dillmann cites 1 Enoch 10:15 here**, with 67:8 and 67:10; 2) *oblectamentum* — a plaything, an object of amusement; 3) *ludus vel lusus cujusvis generis* — a game or sport of any kind; a) *musica, saltatio, chorus* — music, dance, the chorus. **All printed. None preferred.**
- `ገፍዕዎሙ` — Dillmann's `ግፍዕ`: *oppressio, vis, violentia, injuria*; renders both ἀδικία and ἁρπαγή. **He cites 1 Enoch 10:16, 10:20 and 13:2 for the noun.**

**Witnesses:**
- ⭐⭐⭐ **The class being destroyed is different in the two texts.** Ge'ez `ነፍሳተ፡ **ተውኔት**` — *souls of lust/play*, an **abstract quality**. Gizeh `τὰ πνεύματα τῶν **κιβδήλων**` — *the spirits of the **base-born / counterfeit ones***, a **class of persons**. 🛑 And `κιβδήλους` is precisely the word Gizeh used at **10:9** to render Ge'ez `ምኑናን`. So the Greek repeats a term from six verses earlier, and **the Ge'ez does not repeat `ምኑናን` — it introduces a wholly different noun.** One of the two versions has levelled its vocabulary; there is no way to say which. **Both printed.**
- ⚠️ Ge'ez `ወለውሉዶሙ**ኒ**፡ ለትጉሃን` carries the additive enclitic `-ni` ("**and also** the sons of the Watchers"); Gizeh `καὶ τοὺς υἱοὺς τῶν ἐγρηγόρων` is a plain conjunction.
- ⚠️ Ge'ez gives the reason as a **finite clause** with an explicit subject (`እስመ፡ ገፍዕዎሙ` — "**because they** oppressed"); Gizeh uses an **articular infinitive** (`διὰ τὸ ἀδικῆσαι` — "on account of the wronging"), which leaves the agent unstated.
- `p` `Bertalotto` read ወአኅጕሎሙ, and drop the enclitic: ወለውሉዶሙ.
- Qumran Aramaic — **ABSENT**. Latin Fragments — **ABSENT**.

### 10:16

**ወአህጕል፡ ኵሎ፡ ግፍዐ፡ እምገጸ፡ ምድር፡ ወኵሉ፡ ምግባር፡ እኩይ፡ ይኅልቅ፡ ወያስተርኢ፡ ተክለ፡ ጽድቅ፡ ወርትዕ፡ ለዓለም፡ ወይከውን፡ ለበረከት፡ ግብር፡ ጽድቅ፡ ወርትዕ፡ ለዓለም፡ በፍሥሓ፡ ይተከሉ።**

*wäʾähəgwələ kwəlo gəfəʿä ʾəməgäṣä mədərə wäkwəlu məgəbarə ʾəkuyə yəḫələqə wäyasətärəʾi täkəlä ṣədəqə wärətəʿə läʿalämə wäyəkäwənə läbäräkätə gəbərə ṣədəqə wärətəʿə läʿalämə bäfəśəḥa yətäkälu*

**"And destroy all (oppression | violence | force | injury | rapine) from the face of the earth; and let every evil work come to an end; and let the plant of (righteousness | truth) and (uprightness | rectitude | truth) appear for ever; and it shall be for a blessing — works of righteousness and uprightness shall be planted for ever with (joy | gladness | exultation)."**

**Witnesses:**
- ⭐ **Ge'ez has a clause Gizeh lacks**: `ወይከውን፡ **ለበረከት**` — "and it shall be **for a blessing**." No Greek counterpart. ⚠️ It sits in the middle of a Ge'ez passage that also **repeats** `ጽድቅ፡ ወርትዕ፡ ለዓለም` — righteousness-and-uprightness-for-ever — **twice**, where Gizeh has it once (`τὸ φυτὸν τῆς δικαιοσύνης καὶ τῆς ἀληθείας εἰς τοὺς αἰῶνας`). 🛑 `p` `Bertalotto` have the **first** `ለዓለም` absent, reading `ተክለ፡ ጽድቅ፡ ወርትዕ፡ ወይከውን፡ ለበረከት፤ግብር፡ ጽድቅ፡ ወርትዕ፡ ለዓለም`. So the base text alone has it twice: the manuscripts have it once and the Greek has it once. **Base-text doubling, recorded not emended.**
- ⚠️ **Ge'ez `ግፍዐ` against Gizeh `τὴν ἀδικίαν`.** Dillmann's `ግፍዕ` covers *oppressio, vis, violentia, injuria*; `ἀδικία` is the word Gizeh used at 9:1 and 9:6 for Ge'ez `ዐመፃ`. So the Greek uses **one** word where the Ge'ez distinguishes **two** (`ዐመፃ` and `ግፍዕ`). ⭐ This matters at 10:20, where the Ge'ez lists both in the same sentence.
- ⚠️ Gizeh has `μετὰ χαρᾶς φυτευθήσεται` — **singular** passive, "it shall be planted with joy." Ge'ez `በፍሥሓ፡ **ይተከሉ**` — **plural**, "**they** shall be planted." Different subject.
- `p` አኅጕል, የሐልቅ / `Bertalotto` አህጕል, የኀልቅ — ⚠️ note both manuscripts read the imperfect **`የሐልቅ`/`የኀልቅ`** against the base's jussive/subjunctive `ይኅልቅ`.
- `p` `Bertalotto` ግፍዓ, በፍሥሐ — orthographic.
- Qumran Aramaic — **ABSENT**. Latin Fragments — **ABSENT**.

### 10:17

**ወይእዜኒ፡ ኵሎሙ፡ ጻድቃን፡ ይገንዩ፡ ወይከውኑ፡ ሕያዋነ፡ እስከ፡ ይወልዱ፡ ዐሠርተ፡ ምእት፡ ወኵሎ፡ መዋዕለ፡ ውርዙቶሙ፡ ወሰንበተ፡ ዚኣሆሙ፡ ይፌጽሙ፡ በሰላም።**

*wäyəʾəzeni kwəlomu ṣadəqanə yəgänəyu wäyəkäwənu ḥəyawanä ʾəsəkä yəwälədu ʿäśärətä məʾətə wäkwəlo mäwaʿəlä wərəzutomu wäsänəbätä ziʾahomu yəfeṣəmu bäsälamə*

**"And now all the righteous shall (bow down | be humble | submit | render thanks | worship), and they shall be living until they beget a thousand; and all the days of their youth and their sabbaths they shall complete in peace."**

- `ይገንዩ` — `ገነየ` spans **(bow down | be humble | submit | render thanks | worship)**. ⚠️ `geezroot.py` shows the form occurs **only twice in the whole book** — here and at 5:8 — and at 5:8 the Greek likewise has no corresponding clause. **The Ge'ez never disambiguates it and no witness ever helps.**

**Witnesses:**
- ⭐⭐⭐ **The two texts say opposite kinds of thing about the righteous.** Ge'ez `ኵሎሙ፡ ጻድቃን፡ **ይገንዩ**` — they **submit / bow / give thanks**. Gizeh `πάντες οἱ δίκαιοι **ἐκφεύξονται**` — they shall **escape**. 🛑 And `ἐκφεύγω` is the exact verb Gizeh used at **10:3** to render Ge'ez `ይንፍጽ` (escape). So the Greek uses one escape-verb twice; the Ge'ez uses an escape-verb at 10:3 and a **submission-verb** here. One version has a posture, the other a deliverance. **Neither is preferred, and no emendation is proposed.**
- ⚠️ **The number differs in grammatical number.** Ge'ez `ዐሠርተ፡ ምእት` — *ten hundred*, i.e. **a thousand**, singular (`p` `Bertalotto` write it as the numeral `፲፻`). Gizeh `χιλιάδας` — **thousands**, plural.
- `p` `Bertalotto` ዚአሆሙ — orthographic.
- Qumran Aramaic — **ABSENT**. Latin Fragments — **ABSENT**.

### 10:18

**ወበእማንቱ፡ መዋዕል፡ ትትገበር፡ ኵላ፡ ምድር፡ በጽድቅ፡ ወኵለንታሃ፡ ትተከል፡ ዕፀወ፡ ወትመልእ፡ በረከተ።**

*wäbäʾəmanətu mäwaʿələ tətəgäbärə kwəla mədərə bäṣədəqə wäkwəlänətaha tətäkälə ʿəṣ́äwä wätəmäləʾə bäräkätä*

**"And in those days the whole earth shall be (worked | tilled | wrought) in righteousness, and the whole of her shall be planted with (trees | wood | plants), and she shall be filled with blessing."**

**Witnesses:**
- ⭐ **The Greek plants one tree; the Ge'ez plants the whole earth.** Ge'ez `ወ**ኵለንታሃ**፡ ትተከል፡ ዕፀወ` — "and **her entirety** shall be planted with trees." Gizeh `καὶ φυτευθήσεται **δένδρον** ἐν αὐτῇ` — "and **a tree** shall be planted **in her**," **singular**, with no equivalent of `ኵለንታሃ`. Given that "the plant of righteousness" was singular two verses earlier, the Greek singular here is not obviously an error — but neither is the Ge'ez plural. **Both printed.**
- ⚠️ Ge'ez opens `ወበእማንቱ፡ መዋዕል` ("and in those days"); Gizeh `τότε` ("then"). The same connective split as at 10:13/10:14.
- `p` `Bertalotto` agree with the base text throughout this verse.
- Qumran Aramaic — **ABSENT**. Latin Fragments — **ABSENT**.

### 10:19

**ወኵሎ፡ ዕፀወ፡ ሐሤት፡ ይተክሉ፡ ዲቤሃ፡ ወይተክሉ፡ ዲቤሃ፡ አውያነ፡ ወወይን፡ ዘይተከል፡ ዲቤሃ፡ ይገብር፡ ፍሬ፡ ለጽጋብ፡ ወኵሉ፡ ዘርዕ፡ ዘይዘራዕ፡ ዲቤሃ፡ ወአሐቲ፡ መስፈርት፡ ትገብር፡ እልፈ፡ ወአሐቲ፡ መስፈርተ፡ ኤልያስ፡ ትገብር፡ ዐሠርተ፡ ምክያዳተ፡ ዘይት።**

*wäkwəlo ʿəṣ́äwä ḥäśetə yətäkəlu dibeha wäyətäkəlu dibeha ʾäwəyanä wäwäyənə zäyətäkälə dibeha yəgäbərə fəre läṣəgabə wäkwəlu zärəʿə zäyəzäraʿə dibeha wäʾäḥäti mäsəfärətə təgäbərə ʾələfä wäʾäḥäti mäsəfärətä ʾeləyasə təgäbərə ʿäśärətä məkəyadatä zäyətə*

**"And all trees of (joy | gladness | exultation) they shall plant upon her; and they shall plant upon her vines; and the vine that is planted upon her shall bear fruit unto (satiety | abundance | fullness); and all seed that is sown upon her — and one measure shall yield ten thousand; and one measure of ʾElyas [= ἐλαίας, *of olive*] shall yield ten (pressings | treadings) of oil."**

**Witnesses:**
- ⭐⭐⭐ **`ኤልያስ` = `ἐλαίας` — see Finding 5.** The Ge'ez has read the Greek genitive of "olive" as the personal name Elias. `p` `Bertalotto` read `ኤልያሰ`, inflecting the misread noun as an accusative. **The error is in the whole Ge'ez tradition, not one manuscript.**
- ⭐⭐ **`ምክያዳተ` (pressings, treadings) against Gizeh `βάτους` (baths, a measure).** A second Greek-to-Ge'ez artifact in the same clause: a unit of liquid volume has become an action of treading out. ⚠️ Ge'ez `ዐሠርተ` and Gizeh `δέκα` — **the number ten agrees**, which is what makes the two nouns comparable at all.
- ⭐⭐ **The joy is an attribute in one text and a verb in the other.** Ge'ez `ዕፀወ፡ **ሐሤት**` — *trees **of gladness***, a construct chain. Gizeh `πάντα τὰ δένδρα **τῆς γῆς ἀγαλλιάσονται**` — *all the trees **of the earth shall exult***, a genitive plus a finite verb. 🛑 One witness has the joy inside the noun phrase, the other has it as the main predicate and puts "of the earth" in the genitive slot. This is the shape a Semitic construct takes when a Greek translator resolves it — but it can also be a Ge'ez translator collapsing a Greek clause. **Direction not determined.**
- ⭐ **The yields do not match.** Ge'ez `ትገብር፡ **እልፈ**` — *ten thousand / a myriad*. Gizeh `ποιήσουσιν προχοῦς οἴνου **χιλιάδας**` — *thousands of jars of wine*. The Ge'ez has no equivalent of `προχοῦς οἴνου` (jars of wine) and the Greek has no equivalent of the bare `እልፍ`.
- ⚠️ Ge'ez `ወኵሉ፡ ዘርዕ፡ ዘይዘራዕ፡ ዲቤሃ` is left **grammatically dangling** — a nominative with no predicate, resumed by a fresh sentence about measures. Gizeh `καὶ σπόρου ποιήσει καθ᾽ ἕκαστον μέτρον` supplies a verb. Recorded as it stands.
- ⚠️ `p` `Bertalotto` omit the `ወ` before `አሐቲ፡ መስፈርት`, read `ይተከሉ` for the first `ይተክሉ`, and write the numeral `፲` for `ዐሠርተ`.
- `p` ዕጸወ / `Bertalotto` ዕፀወ; `p` `Bertalotto` ኃሤት, ዘርእ, ዘይዘራእ — orthographic.
- Qumran Aramaic — **ABSENT**. Latin Fragments — **ABSENT**.

### 10:20

**ወአንተ፡ አንጽሓ፡ ለምድር፡ እምኵሉ፡ ግፍዕ፡ ወእምኵሉ፡ ዐመፃ፡ ወእምኵሉ፡ ኀጢአት፡ ወእምኵሉ፡ ረሲዕ፡ ወእምኵሉ፡ ርኵስ፡ ዘይትገበር፡ በዲበ፡ ምድር፡ ወአኅልቆሙ፡ እምዲበ፡ ምድር።**

*wäʾänətä ʾänəṣəḥa lämədərə ʾəməkwəlu gəfəʿə wäʾəməkwəlu ʿämäṣ́a wäʾəməkwəlu ḫäṭiʾätə wäʾəməkwəlu räsiʿə wäʾəməkwəlu rəkwəsə zäyətəgäbärə bädibä mədərə wäʾäḫələqomu ʾəmədibä mədərə*

**"And do thou cleanse the earth from all (oppression | violence | injury | rapine), and from all (injustice | iniquity | wickedness | lawlessness), and from all sin, and from all (impiety | ungodliness | wickedness), and from all uncleanness that is done upon the earth; and make an end of them from off the earth."**

**Witnesses:**
- ⭐⭐ **The Ge'ez lists five categories; Gizeh lists four, and they do not begin with the same one.**

  | slot | Ge'ez | Gizeh |
  |---|---|---|
  | 1 | `ግፍዕ` oppression/violence | `ἀκαθαρσίας` uncleanness |
  | 2 | `ዐመፃ` injustice | `ἀδικίας` injustice ✔ |
  | 3 | `ኀጢአት` sin | `ἁμαρτίας` sin ✔ |
  | 4 | `ረሲዕ` impiety | `ἀσεβείας` impiety ✔ |
  | 5 | `ርኵስ` uncleanness | *(moved to the closing clause: `πάσας τὰς ἀκαθαρσίας τὰς γινομένας`)* |

  ⭐ Slots 2–4 agree word for word. **The Greek has `ἀκαθαρσία` at the head of the list and again at the end; the Ge'ez has `ግፍዕ` at the head and `ርኵስ` at the end.** Since Gizeh renders both `ዐመፃ` and `ግፍዕ` with `ἀδικία` elsewhere in this chapter (see 10:16), the Greek has one word for what the Ge'ez keeps as two — and has apparently filled the vacated first slot with a repetition of its own last item. 🛑 That is a description of the two lists, **not a reconstruction of either.**
- ⚠️ **The object of the final verb is masculine plural in Ge'ez** — `ወአኅልቆ**ሙ**` — which cannot straightforwardly refer back to the abstract nouns just listed. Gizeh's `ἐξάλειψον` takes the feminine `τὰς ἀκαθαρσίας`. The Ge'ez suffix points at **persons**; the Greek object is **things**. Recorded; **not resolved.**
- ⚠️ `p` `Bertalotto` read `አንጽ**ሐ**` for base `አንጽሓ`, and `ወ**እን**ኵሉ` (a slip for `ወእምኵሉ`) before `ረሲዕ`.
- `p` አሕልቆሙ / `Bertalotto` አኅልቆሙ; `p` `Bertalotto` ዓመፃ, ኃጢአት — orthographic.
- Qumran Aramaic — **ABSENT**. Latin Fragments — **ABSENT**.

### 10:21

**ወይኩኑ፡ ኵሎሙ፡ ውሉደ፡ ሰብእ፡ ጻድቃነ፡ ወይኩኑ፡ ኵሎሙ፡ አሕዛብ፡ ያምልኩ፡ ወይባርኩ፡ ኪያየ፡ ወኵሎሙ፡ ሊተ፡ ይሰግዱ።**

*wäyəkunu kwəlomu wəludä säbəʾə ṣadəqanä wäyəkunu kwəlomu ʾäḥəzabə yamələku wäyəbarəku kiyayä wäkwəlomu litä yəsägədu*

**"And all the children of (humankind | men) shall be righteous, and all the (peoples | nations | gentiles) shall (serve | worship) and bless me, and all shall bow down to me."**

**Witnesses:**
- ⭐ **Ge'ez has a whole opening clause the Greek lacks**: `ወይኩኑ፡ ኵሎሙ፡ ውሉደ፡ ሰብእ፡ ጻድቃነ` — "and all the children of men shall be **righteous**." Gizeh 10:21 begins directly at `καὶ ἔσονται πάντες λατρεύοντες οἱ λαοὶ`. **No Greek counterpart at all.**
- ⭐⭐ **The three verbs of worship agree exactly, in order** — an unusually clean correspondence for this chapter:

  | Ge'ez | Gizeh |
  |---|---|
  | `ያምልኩ` serve/worship | `λατρεύοντες` |
  | `ወይባርኩ` bless | `εὐλογοῦντες` |
  | `ይሰግዱ` bow down/prostrate | `προσκυνοῦντες` |

  ⚠️ But the **grammar** differs throughout: Ge'ez has three finite verbs; Gizeh has three participles governed by one `ἔσονται`.
- ⚠️ `p` `Bertalotto` read `ኵሉ` (sing.) for the first `ኵሎሙ`, `ያምልክ` (sing.) for `ያምልኩ`, and `ይስግዱ` for `ይሰግዱ`. ⭐ The manuscripts' singular `ያምልክ` breaks agreement with `ኵሎሙ፡ አሕዛብ` and is followed by an **interrogative mark** (`፧`) in the manuscript punctuation — recorded as it stands.
- Qumran Aramaic — **ABSENT**. Latin Fragments — **ABSENT**.

### 10:22

**ወትነጽሕ፡ ምድር፡ እምኵሉ፡ ሙስና፡ ወእምኵሉ፡ ኀጢአት፡ ወእምኵሉ፡ መቅሠፍት፡ ወእምኵሉ፡ ጻዕር፡ ወኢይደግም፡ እንከ፡ እፈኑ፡ ዲቤሃ፡ አይኀ፡ ለትውልደ፡ ትውልድ፡ ወእስከ፡ ለዓለም።**

*wätənäṣəḥə mədərə ʾəməkwəlu musəna wäʾəməkwəlu ḫäṭiʾätə wäʾəməkwəlu mäqəśäfətə wäʾəməkwəlu ṣaʿərə wäʾiyədägəmə ʾənəkä ʾəfänu dibeha ʾäyəḫä lätəwələdä təwələdə wäʾəsəkä läʿalämə*

**"And the earth shall be cleansed from all (corruption | destruction | ruin), and from all sin, and from all (scourge | plague | chastisement), and from all (travail | anguish | torment); and I will not again send a flood upon her, unto generation of generations and unto eternity."**

**Witnesses:**
- ⭐⭐ **The Greek does not say what is sent, and does not send it to the same place.** Ge'ez `ወኢይደግም፡ እንከ፡ እፈኑ፡ **ዲቤሃ**፡ **አይኀ**` — "I will not again send **upon her** [the earth, fem.] **a flood**." Gizeh `καὶ οὐκέτι πέμψω **ἐπ᾽ αὐτοὺς**` — "and I will send no more **upon them**" [masc. pl.], **with no object at all.** 🛑 Two divergences in one clause: the Ge'ez names the flood and the Greek names nothing; the Ge'ez sends it at the earth and the Greek at a group of persons. **Both printed.**
- ⭐ **The four-item list is offset by one.**

  | slot | Ge'ez | Gizeh |
  |---|---|---|
  | 1 | `ሙስና` corruption/defilement | `μιάσματος` defilement ✔ |
  | 2 | `ኀጢአት` **sin** | `ἀκαθαρσίας` **uncleanness** |
  | 3 | `መቅሠፍት` **scourge/plague** | `ὀργῆς` **wrath** |
  | 4 | `ጻዕር` **travail/anguish** | `μάστιγος` **scourge** |

  ⭐ Gizeh's slot 4 (`μάστιξ`, scourge) is the natural equivalent of the Ge'ez's slot **3** (`መቅሠፍት`, scourge). The lists share three of four concepts but **not in the same order**, and each has one item the other does not (`ኀጢአት` sin / `ὀργή` wrath). Recorded as a description of the two lists.
- ⚠️ `p` `Bertalotto` read `ወኢይደግም፡ **ከመ**፡ እፈኑ` (with the complementiser) against base `ወኢይደግም፡ **እንከ**፡ እፈኑ` ("not **again**"). One letter changes "I will not again send" into "I will not repeat, that I should send."
- ⚠️ Gizeh has **no equivalent of `ወእስከ፡ ለዓለም`** as a separate phrase, folding it into `εἰς πάσας τὰς γενεὰς τοῦ αἰῶνος`; and Gizeh adds `πᾶσα` to the earth (`πᾶσα ἡ γῆ`), which the Ge'ez lacks here.
- `p` ፃዕር / `Bertalotto` ጻዕር; `p` `Bertalotto` ኃጢአት — orthographic.
- Qumran Aramaic — **ABSENT**. Latin Fragments — **ABSENT**.
