- **`ethiopic/ethiopian-bible`** — Ge'ez text with word-level transliteration.

  **Eusebius's *Historia Ecclesiastica***.

## Pre-1820 printed editions — `early-editions/scans/`

OCR text layers of public-domain printed editions, retrieved from the **Internet
Archive** (archive.org). Scanning by the Internet Archive and its partner libraries,
including **Google Books**, the **Bayerische Staatsbibliothek**, and the
**Digital Library of India**. Item identifiers in `docs/PULLABLE.tsv`.

Editions include Walton's London Polyglot (1657), the Complutensian (1514–17) and
Antwerp (1568–73) Polyglots, Bomberg's Rabbinic Bibles (1517, 1524–25), Kennicott
(1776–80), de Rossi (1784–88), Holmes & Parsons (1798–1827), the Sixtine LXX (1587),
Grabe's LXX (1707–20), Erasmus (1516), Beza (1565, 1598), and Fabricius's
*Codex Apocryphus Novi Testamenti* (1703/19).

> ⚠️ **OCR quality on Greek and Hebrew is poor** and measurably so — Acts 2:27 comes
> out of the Erasmus layer as `ὅτὶ Ὀυὰ τγκαταλείψας`, and the facing Latin column was
> processed through a Greek model. Usable for Latin prose and apparatus reference
> numbers; not a translation base.

## This project's own work

The tools in `tools/`, the analysis in `HIGHLIGHTS.md`, `EXCLUDED-BOOKS.md`,
`PROVENANCE.md`, `SOURCES.md`, `docs/DATED-INVENTORY.md`, the 20 `docs/RESEARCH-*.md`
reports, and the translations in `translations/`.

The translation in `translations/eusebius-HE-3.25.md` is **this project's own
rendering and has not been checked against a published edition.** Lake's Loeb (1926)
and McGiffert's NPNF (1890) are both public domain and available for comparison.

Every factual claim in `HIGHLIGHTS.md` ships with the command that regenerates it
from the sources. Where a result is uncertain, contested, or rests on a single
witness, it is labelled as such.
